# How to Configure Alerts

The alert feature is used to notify the BioStar administrator when specific device/door/zone/authentication event occurs.

The Alert history can be accessed through **BioStar 2** → **Monitoring** → **Alert History**.

Or by clicking on the alert count located on the bottom right corner of the BioStar web client.

<Image src='/img/how-to/2xtraining_alert001.png' width='1000' alone />

BioStar 2 alert messages get displayed for administrative level users in three ways.

1. **BioStar 2** → **Monitoring** → **Real-time Log**

   The specified event will be colored red on the real-time log.

   :::info
   
   The color of the alert message cannot be customized.

   :::

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2xtraining_alert002.png' width='1000' alone />

1. A popup message on the screen of BioStar 2.

   An alert popup message appears, and then the administrator can either click acknowledge or ignore.

   :::info
   
   Alert Message can be customized: follow **Configuring the Popup Message** at the bottom of this article.

   :::

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2xtraining_alert003.png' width='400' alone />

1. An alert sound Particular alert sound will be played at the occurrence of the event.

   :::info

   The sound feature is only available BioStar 2 version 2.3 or above.

   :::

## Configuring the Real-time Log Event Message

1. Click on **Setting** → **ALERT**.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2xtraining_alert004.png' width='700' alone />

1. Click on the checkbox for the events you want alerts for and click **Apply**.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2xtraining_alert005.png' width='1000' alone />

1. Now the checked event will appear red on the **Real-time log** when the event occurs.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2xtraining_alert006.png' width='1000' alone />

## Configuring the Popup Message

1. Click **Settings** → **ALERT** to access the **Alert** menu.

1. Click on the document icon on the right side of a particular alert message you want to customize

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2xtraining_alert007.png' width='300' alone />

1. Enter your message and click **Apply** to save the settings.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2xtraining_alert008.png' width='500' alone />

## Configuring the Sound Alert

1. Click **Settings** → **PREFERENCE**.

1. Click **+ Add** button in the **Sound** tab.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2xtraining_alert009.png' width='1000' alone />

1. Browse your PC and add a sound file.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2xtraining_alert010.png' width='800' alone />

1. Click **Add** and then click **Apply**.

1. Click **Settings** → **ALERT**.

1. Click on the document icon of a desired event.

1. On the Sound Name drop box select the sound you uploaded on the preference menu.

1. Select your play option.

:::info

Play Options **Once** will run the sound file once

Play Options **Loop until acknowledge** will run the sound until user clicks acknowledge on the popup menu that appears when the event occurs.

:::
