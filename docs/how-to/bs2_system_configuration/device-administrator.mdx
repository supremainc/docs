# How to Configure a Device Administrator

In BioStar 2, you can configure device administrators who have different privileges.

It is also necessary to configure a device administrator to lock the device menu from being accessed by all other users.

## Configuring an Administrator to Lock the Menu

1. Click on the **DEVICE** menu.

1. Click on the device you would like to configure.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2x_training_device_administrator001.png' width='1000' alone />

1. Scroll down to the **Advanced** tab.

1. On the **All** item click **+ Add**.

1. Select a user.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2x_training_device_administrator002.png' width='800' alone />

1. Click **Apply** at the bottom of the page.

Check that the device menu is locked. Only the device administrator will be able to access the menu after inputting credentials.

:::warning

The administrator menu can be accessible by any user if defined admin is deleted from the device or not transferred to the device. 

:::

:::info

Refer to the administrator's manual regarding the different kinds of device administrators.

Administrator's manual is provided in the **Help** button of BioStar 2. 

:::
