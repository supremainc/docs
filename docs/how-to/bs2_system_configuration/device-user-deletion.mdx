# How to delete users in device through BioStar 2

From BioStar 2.7.1, you can delete users in the device through the server (BioStar 2).

FW should be compatible with BioStar 2.7.1.

:::info

* BioStation 2: 1.7.0 or later

* BioStation A2: 1.6.0 or later

* BioStation L2: 1.4.0 or later

* BioEntry W2: 1.3.0 or later

* FaceStation 2: 1.2.0 or later

* CoreStation: 1.2.0 or later

* BioEntry P2: 1.2.0 or later

* BioLite N2: 1.1.0 or later

* X-Station 2: 1.0.0 or later (From BioStar 2.8.11)

* FaceStation F2: 1.0.0 or later (From BioStar 2.8.6)

* FaceLite: 1.0.0 or later (From BioStar 2.7.10)

* XPass 2: 1.0.0 or later (From BioStar 2.7.10)

:::

1. Select the device you'd like to delete USERS from.

1. Click **Manage USERS in Device** button.

1. Check the USERS you'd like to delete from the device.

1. Click **Delete** button and continue.
