# Increase the Number of Administrators That Can Be Added per Devices

From BioStar 2 v2.7.3, you can configure more admin users within 2nd generation devices.

1. Capability change

   * Before BioStar 2 v2.7.3, you could configure maximum 10 admin users.

   * From BioStar 2 v2.7.3, you can configure maximum 1000 admin users.

1. Compatibility notice

   * BioStar 2 (old version) - Device (new version): guarantees max 10 admin users.

   * BioStar 2 (new version) - Device (old version): cannot guarantee more than 10 admin users.

   * BioStar 2 (new version) - Device (new version): guarantees over than 10 admin users.

1. Supporting device list

   * BioStation 2 1.8.0 or above

   * BioStation A2 1.7.0 or above

   * BioStation L2 1.5.0 or above

   * BioStation N2 1.2.0 or above

   * FaceLite 1.0.1 or above

   * FaceStation 2 1.3.0 or above

   * FaceStation F2 1.0.0 or above

   * X-Station 2 1.0.0 or above

1. Not-supporting device list

   * Entry Devices 2.x

   * Official Release 2.x (old versions)
