# How to edit the report

From BioStar 2.4.1, you can edit the temporary schedule, punch log, and leave.

## Editing the Temporary Schedule

1. Click on the **Schedule** tab in the **Time Attendance** menu.

1. Click on the **+** sign left of the Schedule.

1. Select a user.

1. Left click on a day of the calendar.

1. Click **Add Temporary Schedule**.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2x_sc_editing_ta_report_002.jpg' width='700' alone />

1. Enter a desired **Name** for this temporary schedule.

1. Select a preconfigured **Shift**.

1. Select the Period of time range to apply.

1. Use **Apply to Other Users** option to apply to multiple users.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2x_sc_editing_ta_report_003.jpg' width='600' alone />

## Editing Punch Log

### Option 1

1. Click on the **Report** column in the **Time Attendance menu**.

1. Generate a report by selecting **Period**, **Report Type**, **User Group/Use** and clicking **Update Report**.

1. Click on a row of the report.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2x_sc_editing_ta_report_006.jpg' width='700' alone />

1. Click on the item below the **In / Out** column.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2x_sc_editing_ta_report_007.jpg' width='700' alone />

1. Edit the punch log by clicking on the pencil.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2x_sc_editing_ta_report_008.jpg' width='700' alone />

:::info

Try checking **Rebuild Time Card** and then click **Update Report** if your report seems out of date.

:::

### Option 2

1. Click on the **Report** tab in the **Time Attendance menu**.

1. Generate a report by selecting **Period**, **Report Type**, **User Group/Use** and clicking **Update Report**.

1. Click on a row of the report.

1. Click on the calendar icon on the right top corner.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2x_sc_editing_ta_report_009.jpg' width='100' alone />

1. Click on the **Work Time** (light grey) item of the day.

1. Edit the punch log by clicking on the pencil.

## Editing Leave

### Option 1

1. Click on the **Report** column in the **Time Attendance menu**.

1. Generate a report by selecting **Period**, **Report Type**, **User Group/Use** and clicking **Update Report**.

1. Click on the pencil icon.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2x_sc_editing_ta_report_011.png' width='36' alone />

1. Select a **Leave Time Code**.

1. Check **Use Time** only if there is a desired number of hours that should be applied as leave.

   For instance, a half day leave would be 4 hours.

1. Set **Start Date** and **End Date** if the leave spans over a few days.

1. Use **Apply to Other User** to apply the same setting to multiple users.

1. Leave comments on **Approver Comments** if necessary.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2x_sc_editing_ta_report_012.jpg' width='700' alone />

:::info

The feature to select leave hours (**Use Time**) was introduced in BioStar 2.4.1. 

:::

### Option 2

1. Click on the **Report** tab in the **Time Attendance menu**.

1. Generate a report by selecting **Period**, **Report Type**, **User Group/Use** and clicking **Update Report**.

1. Click on a row of the report.

1. Click on the calendar icon on the right top corner.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2x_sc_editing_ta_report_009.jpg' width='100' alone />

1. Click on the **Shift** (grey) item of the day.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2x_sc_editing_ta_report_013.jpg' width='700' alone />

1. Select a **Leave Time Code**.

1. Check **Use Time** only if there is a desired number of hours that should be applied as leave.

   For instance, a half day leave would be 4 hours.

1. Set **Start Date** and **End Date** if the leave spans over a few days.

1. Use **Apply to Other User** to apply the same setting to multiple users.

1. Leave comments on **Approver Comments** if necessary.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2x_sc_editing_ta_report_012.jpg' width='700' alone />
