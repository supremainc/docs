# How to send Email when a device is disconnected

From BioStar 2.5.0, you can configure an Email about the device disconnection detected. When a device is disconnected, the email will be sent to a recipient you set.

Go to **Setting** → **Trigger & Action**.

<Image src='/img/how-to/2x_sc_email_1.png' width='900' alone />

Select the certain device. Then, check the box named **Device Disconnection Detected** and **Biostar**.

<Image src='/img/how-to/2x_sc_email_2.png' width='1000' alone />

Click <IcSet2/> to configure the email server information. Then, type the information on your email server.

<Image src='/img/how-to/2x_sc_email_3.png' width='1000' alone />

When it comes to setting of SMTP Option, refer to the below screenshot.

Set up your SMTP. If you are unsure what the settings are, please follow this article → [How to configure SMTP and Test the recipient address](https://support.supremainc.com/en/support/solutions/articles/24000081661--biostar-2-how-to-configure-smtp-and-test-the-recipient-address).

<Image src='/img/how-to/device_disconnection_alert_smtp_setting.jpg' width='500' alone />

Add a Recipient and click **Apply** button.

<Image src='/img/how-to/device_disconnection_email_setting.jpg' width='400' alone />

When the device is disconnected, an email titled "\[BioStar Alert\] Device Disconnection Detected" will be set to the recipient you add.

<Image src='/img/how-to/device_disconnection_alert.jpg' width='400' alone />
