# How to Configure Live Video View

Live Video View has been introduced in BioStar 2.6.2. You can now see the live feed from your video camera and also control the door from the live video view menu.

## Configuration

:::info

For in-depth basic video configuration and cautions refer to the following article: [How to use video](video-overview)

:::

1. Click on the **VIDEO** menu.

1. Click **Add New NVR**.

1. Input your NVR details.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2x_sysconfig_live_video_001.png' width='500' alone />

1. Click **Apply**.

1. Click **Add New Camera**.

1. Select your **NVR** and click **Next**.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2x_sysconfig_live_video_002.png' width='700' alone />

1. Select your camera and click **Add**.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2x_sysconfig_live_video_003.png' width='600' alone />

1. Click **All Cameras** and choose your camera to configure a Door.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2x_sysconfig_live_video_004.png' width='1000' alone />

1. Choose a **Door** to assign the camera and click **+ Add** to choose an event to use for the video log.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2x_sysconfig_live_video_005.png' width='1000' alone />

1. Click **Apply**.

1. Go to the **MONITORING** menu's **Live Video View**.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2x_sysconfig_live_video_006.png' width='300' alone />

1. Go to the **MONITORING** menu's **Live Video View**.

1. Click on the dropdown box and choose a camera.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2x_sysconfig_live_video_007.png' width='500' alone />

1. When the load complete the real time video should appear.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2x_sysconfig_live_video_008.png' width='1000' alone />

1. You can also control the door with the door icons visible beside the dropdown box (open, manual unlock, manual lock, release).

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2x_sysconfig_live_video_009.png' width='300' alone />
