# How to assign temporary schedule to an employee

:::info

BioStar 2 TA allows administrator to assign a schedule template to an employee on a temporary basis. The employee will remain on the schedule only for the time period selected.

:::

 1. Click on the **Schedule** tab in the **Time Attendance** menu.

1. Click on the **+** sign left of the Schedule.

1. Select a user.

1. Left click on a day of the calendar.

1. Click **Add Temporary Schedule**.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2x_sc_how_to_assign_a_temporary_schedule_001.png' width='1000' alone />

1. Enter a desired **Name** for this temporary schedule.

1. Select a preconfigured **Shift**.

1. Select the Period of time range to apply.

1. Use **Apply to Other Users** option to apply to multiple users.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2x_sc_how_to_assign_a_temporary_schedule_002.png' width='700' alone />

:::info

The option to apply the temporary schedule to multiple users was introduced in BioStar 2.4. 

:::
