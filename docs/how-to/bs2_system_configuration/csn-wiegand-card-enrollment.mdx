# How to Enroll the CSN and Wiegand cards

You can easily enroll the cards to users in BioStar 2 with a Suprema device or a USB reader (DE-620).

Your device may support Wiegand or CSN cards based on its model type.

Refer to the link to learn about card type support based on device model: [Device Card Read Specification](http://support.supremainc.com/solution/articles/24000006349--biostar-2-supported-card-and-card-formats-based-on-model-type)

:::info

DE-620 is supported by BioStar 2 v2.4.0 and above. 

:::

## Enrolling a CSN Card

1. Click on the **DEVICE** menu of your BioStar 2.

1. Select a device.

1. On the **Authentication** tab's **CSN Card Format** set the **Format Type** as **Normal**.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2x_sc_csn_wiegand_card_enroll_001.png' width='800' alone />

1. Click **Apply**.

1. Click on the **USER** menu of your BioStar 2.

1. Click **ADD USER**.

1. Enter basic user information. (e.g. **Name**, **ID** and etc.)

1. Click **+ Card**.

1. Set **Cart Type** as **CSN**.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2x_sc_csn_wiegand_card_enroll_002.png' width='600' alone />

1. For **Registration Option** choose **Register by Card Reader**.

   :::info

   Other Registration Options:

   **Assign Card**: You will be able to reuse a card that has been enrolled before but was deleted from a user.

   The list of cards that are unassigned can be seen in the **Settings** → **Card** → **Unassigned Card** list.

   **Enter Manually**: Allows you to type in the card ID manually.

   :::

1. Click **Read Card**.

1. Scan the card on the device of your choice.

1. When the card is read successfully, click **Enroll**. 

   :::info

   If you have already enrolled the card previously to another user and deleted the card, an error message will pop up.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2x_sc_csn_wiegand_card_enroll_003.png' width='300' alone />

   In this case you would have to use the **Assign Card** **Registration Option** to search for the CSN card and enroll.

   :::

14. Click **Apply**.

## Enrolling a Wiegand Card

1. Click on the **DEVICE** menu.

1. Select your device.

1. On the **Authentication** tab's **Wiegand Card Format** set your desired **Wiegand Card Format**.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2x_sc_csn_wiegand_card_enroll_004.png' width='700' alone />

   :::info

   You can select multiple Wiegand formats to be read by selecting multiple formats. 

   If you are using a custom Wiegand format that is not listed in the default formats, you can configure your format in **Settings** → **Card Format** → **Wiegand**.

   :::

1. Click **Apply**.

1. Click on the **USER** menu of your BioStar 2.

1. Click **ADD USER**.

1. Enter basic user information such as **Name**, **ID** and etc.

1. Click **+ Card**.

1. Set **Cart Type** as **Wiegand**.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2x_sc_csn_wiegand_card_enroll_005.png' width='600' alone />

1. Select your **Card Data Format**.

1. Select **Registration Option** as **Register by Card Reader**.

1. Select your **Device**.

1. Click **Read Card**.

1. Scan your card on the device.

1. Click **Enroll**.

1. Click **Apply**.
