# How to Configure a T&A Schedule to Calculate or Ignore Break Time

If you would like to keep track or ignore the break time of users during the day, refer to the configuration below.

For a basic step by step guide on fixed and flexible shift configuration, refer to the fixed and flexible shift articles:

[How to create T&A settings for organizations with fixed shift](ta-fixed-shift)

[How to create T&A settings for organizations with flexible shift](ta-flexible-shift)

## Configuring Break Time

1. On your **Shift** page turn off First check-in & Last check-out.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2x_sc_ta_break_time_configuration_01.png' width='600' alone />

1. Turn on **Break by Punch**.

1. Configure **Max. allowed break time** as required.

   :::info

   If the user exceeds the max allowed break time, it will appear in the report as **Over Break Time**.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2x_sc_ta_break_time_configuration_02.png' width='900' alone />

   :::

1. Proceed with **add schedule template**, **add schedule** as required.

1. Go to the device setting menu by pressing the gear icon.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2x_sc_ta_break_time_configuration_03.png' width='25' alone />

1. Select your registered device and click **Setting**.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2x_sc_ta_break_time_configuration_04.png' width='800' alone />

1. Select your **T&A mode** as desired.

   :::info

   Refer to [How to Configure a T&A Device](ta-device-settings) for T&A modes. 

   :::

1. Configure T&A types of **Check In**, **Check Out**, **Break Start**, **Break End**, **Meal Time Start**, **Meal Time End**.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2x_sc_ta_break_time_configuration_05.png' width='1000' alone />

   The user should enter the T&A key in following procedure during the day **Check In** → **Break Start** → **Break End** → **Break Start** → **Break End** → **Check out**.

1. Click **Apply**.

:::info

For devices without T&A keys, refer to [How to calculate exact worktime/breaktime for devices without T&A key?](ta-worktime-without-ta-key).

:::

## Configuring to Ignore Break Time

This configuration will calculate the work time based on the first and last punch of the day.

1. On your **Shift** page, turn on First check-in & Last check-out.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2x_sc_ta_break_time_configuration_06.png' width='600' alone />

1. Turn off **Break by Punch**.

1. Proceed with **add schedule template**, **add schedule** as required.

1. Go to the device setting menu by pressing the gear icon.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2x_sc_ta_break_time_configuration_03.png' width='25' alone />

1. Select your registered device and click **Setting**.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2x_sc_ta_break_time_configuration_04.png' width='800' alone />

1. On T&A Mode select **Not Use** and click **Apply**.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2x_sc_ta_break_time_configuration_07.png' width='300' alone />

   Users will not have to press a T&A button on authentication because the work time is calculated based on the first and last punch of the configured work day.
