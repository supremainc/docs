## How to customize alert message

1. Click **Settings** → **ALERT** to access the **Alert** menu.

1. Click on the document icon on the right side of a particular alert message you want to customize.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2xtraining_alert007.png' width='300' alone />

1. Enter your message and click **Apply** to save the settings.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2xtraining_alert008.png' width='500' alone />

## Alert Monitor and Alert Icon of BioStar2 version 2.7.10 or lower version

<Image src='/img/how-to/11-c.png' width='1000' alone />

From BioStar2 version 2.7.11, Alert list and Alarm Monitor have been improved for better performance of BioStar2 Sofware.

1. Changed icons and phrases.

   * The lower version than BioStar2 version 2.7.10 indicated the number of alerts. BioStar2 version 2.7.11 or higher version indicates an icon instead of showing the number of alerts.

   * The name of **Alarm Monitor** has been changed to **Missed Alarm**.

1. Changed to indicate only the last 15 alarms out of those that have not been checked in the last 6 months.

   For better performance, the list of Missed Alarm only shows the recent not confirmed 15 alarms in the last 6 months.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/11-d.png' width='1000' alone />

1. Changed to indicate all alerts once a user clicks the alert icon.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/11-e.png' width='1000' alone />
