# How to configure Trigger and Action on Setting

The trigger & action feature is used when you would like to send a custom output signal from a device or send an email to the administrator based on the occurrence of an alarm event.

For instance, you can use this feature to trigger your alarm lamp to go on simultaneously when a forced door open alarm goes on. The system can also simultaneously send an email to the administrator as well.

## Setting Up a Custom Signal Output

In the scenario below, we will send a custom signal from BioStation A2 Device when there is a forced opened trigger event.

1. Click **Setting** → **TRIGGER & ACTION**.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2xtraining_trigger_action003.png' width='500' alone />

1. Click **ADD TRIGGER & ACTION**.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2xtraining_trigger_action005.png' width='200' alone />

1. Enter a name for this trigger on the **Name** textbox.

1. Click on the schedule drop box. Click **+ Add Schedule**. If you have a pre-configured schedule, you can use that one by selecting it.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2xtraining_trigger_action006.png' width='1000' alone />

1. Configure the schedule as necessary and click **Ok**. In this case, I would like the trigger to be on all day except for Sunday.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2xtraining_trigger_action008.png' width='1000' alone />

1. Now select the newly created schedule on the **Schedule** drop box.

1. Select the **Door** radio button.

1. Select the door this will affect. The BioStation A2 Device is installed on Door 3 in this example.

1. Check **Forced door opened**.

1. Select your device (BioStation A2).

1. Relay 0 is already used for the door lock so relay 1 will be used for the output.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2xtraining_trigger_action010.png' width='1000' alone />

1. Click on the **Signal** drop down box.

1. Click **Add Signal**.

1. Fill in the details as desired and click **Apply**.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2xtraining_trigger_action012.png' width='600' alone />

1. Click on the **Signal** drop box and select the signal you just created and click **Apply**.

1. Now when there is a forced door open event, the custom signal is sent to relay 1.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2xtraining_trigger_action014.png' width='1000' alone />

## Setting Up an Email Alert on BioStar

1. Click **Setting** → **TRIGGER & ACTION**.

1. Click on your desired trigger.

1. On Device / BioStar tab, click **BioStar** and check **BioStar**.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2xtraining_trigger_action016.png' width='1000' alone />

1. Click on the Gear icon on the action tab to setup your email.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2xtraining_trigger_action018.png' width='27' alone />

1. Set up your SMTP. If you are unsure what the settings are, please follow this article → [How to configure SMTP and Test the recipient address](https://support.supremainc.com/en/support/solutions/articles/24000081661--biostar-2-how-to-configure-smtp-and-test-the-recipient-address).

   <Image src='/img/how-to/how_to_configure_trigger_action_smtp_section.jpg' width='400' alone />

1. Add a **Recipient** and click **Apply**.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/how_to_configure_trigger_action_received_email_section.jpg' width='400' alone />

1. Now create a forced door open event.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/2xtraining_trigger_action024.png' width='1000' alone />

1. The administrator will receive an alert email.

   <Image src='/img/how-to/how_to_configure_trigger_action_image.jpg' width='500' alone />
