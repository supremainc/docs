## Milestone XProtect Smart Client and BioStar

1. Run Milestone XProtect Smart Client. It is retrieving plugin information.

   <Image src='/img/integration/milestone/sync-check1.png' alone />

1. Move **Access Control** tab, Check the Device and door status sync with BioStar setting.

   <Image src='/img/integration/milestone/sync-check2.png' alone />

1. Check the Cardholders' information with BioStar setting.

   <Image src='/img/integration/milestone/sync-check3.png' alone />

   <Image src='/img/integration/milestone/sync-check4.png' alone />

1. When you check the Event on real time, you need to press 'refresh' button.

   <Image src='/img/integration/milestone/sync-check5.png' alone />
