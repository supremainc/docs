## Card Enrollment

1. In the **Config Tool**, click the **Cardholder management** under **Tasks**.

   <Image src='/img/integration/genetec/card-enrollment.png' width={800} alone />

1. Click the **New** button from the bottom.

   <Image src='/img/integration/genetec/new-ico.png' alone />

1. Enter the **First name** and **Last name**, then click the **Add a credential** button. In the popup that appears, click **Automatic entry**.

   <Image src='/img/integration/genetec/card-enrollment-add-credential1.png' width={800} alone />

1. Select the **Door** assigned to the Suprema device as a reader, then tag a card to the device.

   <Image src='/img/integration/genetec/card-enrollment-add-credential2.png' width={800} alone />

1. The card is automatically enrolled to the credential.

   <Image src='/img/integration/genetec/card-enrollment-add-credential3.png' width={800} alone />

## Face Enrollment

### Precautions when Enrolling Face

When enrolling your face for authentication, refer to the picture below to enroll your face correctly.

If you do not follow the on-screen instructions, face enrollment may take a long time or authentication may fail.

* When enrolling a face, maintain a distance of 60-100 cm between the device and the face.

* Make sure that your expression does not change.

* Do not wear a mask, hat, sunglasses, or eye patches.

* Do not raise head up or lower head.

* Do not wear thick makeup.

* Do not close your eyes.

* Make sure that both of your shoulders correctly appear on the screen.

* Do not use a corrected picture.

* Stand still and enroll your face by staring at the screen.

* Be careful not to display two faces on the screen. Make sure to enroll one face at a time.

* Do not enroll your face in dark places.

<Image src='/img/integration/genetec/face-enrollment.png' width={800} alone />

:::info

* **Resolution**: Larger than 250 x 250 pixels

* **Image size**: Up to 10 MB

* **Supported format**: JPG, JPEG or PNG only

:::

### Enroll from Device

1. In the cardholder details window, click **Suprema Enrollment**.

   <Image src='/img/integration/genetec/enroll-from-device1.png' width={800} alone />

1. Click the **Face-Scan**.

   <Image src='/img/integration/genetec/enroll-from-device2.png' width={800} alone />

1. Select the Suprema face authentication device to enroll the face credential.

   <Image src='/img/integration/genetec/enroll-from-device3.png' alone />

1. Click the **+Add** button to create a face template, then click the **Scan** button and scan the face on the device.

   <Image src='/img/integration/genetec/enroll-from-device4.png' alone />

1. Check the face has been properly scanned, and then click the **Enroll** button to enroll the face.

### Enroll Face Template with Genetec Profile Photo

1. In the cardholder details window, click **Suprema Enrollment**.

   <Image src='/img/integration/genetec/enroll-from-device1.png' width={800} alone />

1. Click the **Face-Scan**.

   <Image src='/img/integration/genetec/enroll-from-device2.png' width={800} alone />

1. Select the Suprema face authentication device to enroll the face credential.

   <Image src='/img/integration/genetec/enroll-from-device3.png' alone />

1. Click the **+Add** button to create a face template, then check **Use Genetec Photo** to automatically upload the user profile photo. The Suprema device extracts face templates from user profile photos.

   <Image src='/img/integration/genetec/enroll-from-device5.png' alone />

1. Check the uploaded photo, and then click the **Enroll** button to enroll the face.

## Bulk Enrollment

1. Go to **Suprema Device Plugin** → **Global Settings**.

   <Image src='/img/integration/genetec/global-settings1.png' alone />

1. Click the **Bulk Face Upload** button. You can check the Face Extraction progress.

   <Image src='/img/integration/genetec/global-settings2.png' alone />

   :::info
   
   Face template extraction is performed only for GSC cardholders who have a profile image.

   :::

1. Once the process is completed, you can check the results.

   * Show Failed Users: View the list of users for whom face extraction failed.

   * Face Template Extraction Report: View the report related to face template extraction.

## How to Search for a Cardholder with a Face Template

1. Click the **Advanced search** on the **Cardholder management** page.

   <Image src='/img/integration/genetec/advanced-search.png' width={500} alone />

1. Click the **Custom fields**, switch it to **On**, and click **Add criterion**. Then, select the **BiometricsTimestamp**.

   <Image src='/img/integration/genetec/advanced-search-custom-fields1.png' width={500} alone />

1. Select the **Specific range** and set the period to search.

   :::note
   
   **Example**

   * **From**: Plugin installation date

   * **To**: Today

   :::

   To search by specific time, click **Set time** and enter the time.

   <Image src='/img/integration/genetec/advanced-search-custom-fields2.png' alone />

1. Click the **Search** button.

1. All cardholders who have face templates appear.

## Private Authentication Mode

Set a different authentication mode for each user, in addition to or instead of the device’s default authentication mode.

1. In the **Enrolment Credentials** window, click **Private Auth**.

   <Image src='/img/integration/genetec/enrolment-credentials-private-auth.png' width={800} alone />

2. In the **Private Auth Mode** window, turn on **Private Mode**.

   <Image src='/img/integration/genetec/private-auth-mode1.png' width={900} alone />

3. Click **+ Add**. The **Add New Auth Mode** window appears.

4. Set **Extended Auth Mode** if needed.

   <Image src='/img/integration/genetec/private-auth-mode2.png' width={900} alone />

   * **Not Use**: **Biometrics** (fingerprint or face) is used as one credential.

   * **Use**: **Fingerprint** and **Face / Visual Face** can be combined as separate credentials.

5. Drag the desired credentials (**Biometrics**, **Fingerprint**, **Face / Visual Face**, **Card**, **ID**, **PIN**) to the center area to make an authentication mode, then click **OK**.

6. Set **Include Device Default Authentication Mode**.

   * ON: Both the device default authentication modes and the private authentication modes are used.

   * OFF: Only the private authentication modes are used.

7. Check the added modes in the **Auth Mode** list, then click **Save**.

:::info

**Extended Auth Mode** is only supported by FaceStation F2, BioStation 3, and BioEntry W3.

:::

## AoC Enrollment (Access On Card)

Select Access On Card to store user information on the card. This includes card ID, PIN, access group, period, fingerprint template, face template, and private authentication mode.

Before issuing, configure the smart card format (Smart Card Setting) and apply it to the device used for enrollment.

1. In the **Enrolment Credentials** window, click **AoC Enrollment**.

   <Image src='/img/integration/genetec/enrolment-credentials-aoc-enrollment.png' width={800} alone />

2. When the **Enroll Card** window appears, select Smart Card from the **Card Type** options.

   <Image src='/img/integration/genetec/aoc-enroll-card.png' width={900} alone />

3. Select the device for card enrollment from the **Device** field.

4. Once selected, the card layout format set on the device is displayed in the **Card Layout Format** field.

5. Select **Access On Card** from the **Smart Card Type** options.

6. Select a fingerprint or face template if available. If no template is available, skip this step.

   * **Fingerprint Template on Card**: Check **Use**, then check **Select** for each finger to write. Check **Duress** for a duress fingerprint.

      <Image src='/img/integration/genetec/aoc-enroll-card1.png' width={900} alone />

   * **Face Template on Card**: Check **Use**, then check *Select* for the face template to write.

      <Image src='/img/integration/genetec/aoc-enroll-card2.png' width={900} alone />

7. (Optional) **Private Authentication Mode**: Click **+ Add** to add authentication rules written to the smart card.

   <Image src='/img/integration/genetec/aoc-enroll-card3.png' width={900} alone />

8. Click the **Write Smart Card** button.

:::info

* The cardholder must have an access rule and biometric data (fingerprint or face) enrolled before an **Access On Card** can be issued.

* **Access On Card** cannot set the card ID manually.

* The **Fingerprint Template on Card** and **Face Template on Card** sections follow the card layout (Template Count, Use Face Template).

:::
