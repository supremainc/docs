August brings a wealth of news to **Suprema Docs**, from a range of device release notes to new developer documentation.

The new firmware (version 1.5.0) for **BioStation 3** adds support for a variety of features, including **XPass Q2** integration, sequential multi-face authentication, and Anti-Tailgating. Release notes for the new firmware of **BioEntry W3**, **BioEntry W2**, **BioLite N2**, **BioStation 2a**, **XPass 2**, **XPass D2**, and **FaceStation F2** have also been updated. Notably, the release notes for **XPass Q2** are published for the first time this month.

In the **Developers** section, we have also newly released the **ViOnyx AI Camera API** documentation—a REST + WebRTC–based interface for live video, AI events, face authentication, and device settings management on the **ViOnyx** AI camera. Take a look now.
