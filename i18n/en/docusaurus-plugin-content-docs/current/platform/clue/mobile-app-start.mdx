With the CLUe mobile app, control doors remotely from your smartphone, manage users, and check place events in real time. This guide covers everything from how to invite place managers to downloading the app, signing in, and using the main features.

## Inviting CLUe mobile app admins

A place group admin can invite place admins by email in Place settings.

1. Log in to the [CLUe web portal](https://clue.moon.supremainc.com).

2. Select the place where you want to invite a mobile app admin.

3. In the left sidebar, click <Cmd sid='settings' product='clue' /> → <Cmd sid='admin' product='clue' />.

   <ProdImg src='/img/clue/place-invite-admin.png' />

4. Enter the email address and select a role.

5. Click the <Cmd sid='invite' product='clue' /> button.

   <ProdImg src='/img/clue/place-mobile-admin-invited.png' />

   Check the invitation status in the <Cmd sid='admin_list' product='clue' /> list.

After the invitation completes, the system sends an invitation email to the admin. The invited admin can join through the link in the email.

<ProdImg src='/img/clue/clue-invite-email.png' />

After joining, check the status change in the admin list.

<ProdImg src='/img/clue/place-mobile-admin-invite-complete.png' />

:::note

**Can I join directly in the CLUe mobile app?**

Admins who cannot access the CLUe web portal can download the CLUe app and join from the mobile app. However, for actual use, the system must invite them to the place.

:::

## Log in

After installing the app, sign in with the invited email address.

1. Open the CLUe app on your mobile device.

2. Select <Cmd>Login with Email</Cmd>.

   <ProdImg src='/img/clue/clue-app-select-login-method.png' width='250px' />

3. Enter the email address and password, then tap the <Cmd>Login</Cmd> button.

   <ProdImg src='/img/clue/clue-app-email-login.png' width='250px' />

4. Enter the verification code sent to your email address, then tap the <Cmd>Confirm</Cmd> button.

   <ProdImg src='/img/clue/clue-app-email-login-auth-number.png' width='250px' />

You stay signed in until you sign out.
