title: Upgrade Firmware
description: This guide explains how to upgrade a device’s firmware to the latest version in the CLUe web portal.
---

Upgrading the firmware on a CLUe device lets you apply new features, performance improvements, and security patches. The upgrade takes only a few clicks in the CLUe web portal and takes about 5 to 10 minutes to complete.

Before starting the upgrade, check the following:

* You cannot use the device during the upgrade, and when it restarts, the door may briefly open or close depending on the lock connection method.

* If you change device settings during the upgrade, the changes apply after completion.

## Go to a place

To upgrade an access device assigned to a place, go directly to that place, or navigate through the upper Branch, Distributor, or Place group.

1. Log in to the [CLUe web portal](https://clue.moon.supremainc.com).

2. On the initial screen, select a Branch, Distributor, or Place group.

3. Click <IcAirMore title='more' /> → <Cmd sid='settings_2' product='clue' /> in the upper right of the screen.

   <Image src='/img/clue/add-device-settings.png' />

4. Click <Cmd sid='device' product='clue' /> in the left sidebar.

   <Image src='/img/clue/add-device-go-device-tap.png' />

5. In <Cmd sid='device_list' product='clue' />, click the <Cmd sid='place' product='clue' /> column for the device whose firmware you want to upgrade.

   <Image src='/img/clue/add-device-complete.png' />

6. When the <IcCLMove title='move' /> button becomes active, click it.

The selected device moves to the <Cmd sid='device_2' product='clue' /> screen for the assigned place.

## Firmware upgrade

1. In the <Cmd sid='device_2' product='clue' /> list for the assigned place, click the device you want to upgrade.

   <Image src='/img/clue/clue-fw-device-list.png' />

2. When the selected device’s <Cmd sid='basic_settings' product='clue' /> screen appears, check the current firmware version in <Cmd sid='firmware_version' product='clue' />.

   <Image src='/img/clue/clue-fw-device-basic-settings.png' />

3. Click the <Cmd sid='upgrade' product='clue' /> button.

4. When the firmware selection window appears, click the checkbox for the firmware you want to upgrade.

   <Image src='/img/clue/clue-fw-device-basic-select-fw-ver.png' />

   :::note

   You can select only one firmware version.

   :::

5. Click the <Cmd sid='apply' product='clue' /> button in the lower right.

6. When the upgrade confirmation window appears, click the <Cmd sid='process' product='clue' /> button.

   <Image src='/img/clue/clue-fw-device-basic-upgrade-fw-verify.png' />

7. When the message window appears, review the contents, and click the <Cmd sid='ok' product='clue' /> button.

   <Image src='/img/clue/clue-fw-device-basic-upgrade-fw-confirm.png' />
