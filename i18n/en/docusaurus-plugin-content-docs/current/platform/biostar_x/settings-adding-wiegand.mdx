Learn how to add a Wiegand device to a registered master device or slave device.

:::info

* <Glossary termid='master_device' />

* <Glossary termid='slave_device' />

* <Glossary termid='wiegand' />

:::

1. On the <Cmd sid='shell.header.launcher' /> page, click <Cmd sid='shell.header.setting' />.

2. Click <Cmd sid='setting.menu.device.root' /> → <Cmd sid='setting.menu.device.device' /> in the left sidebar.

3. Right-click the master or slave device in the <Cmd sid='menu.device.allDeviceGroups' product='2' /> list.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-setting-device-adding-wiegand.png' />

4. Click <Cmd sid='device.menu.addWiegandDevice' product='2' /> in the pop-up menu.

5. When the list of Wiegand devices connected to the master device appears, select the device to register.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-setting-device-adding-wiegand-list.png' />

6. Click <Cmd sid='button.add' product='2' />.

7. When the device registration confirmation message appears, click <Cmd sid='button.apply' product='2' />.

The registered Wiegand device appears under the selected device.

<Image src='/img/biostarx-setting-device-adding-wiegand-ok.png' />
