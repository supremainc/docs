Delete widgets added to the dashboard.

1. On the <Cmd sid='shell.header.launcher' /> page, click <Cmd sid='shell.header.dashboard' />.

2. Click <IcSet2 /> in the upper-right corner of the screen.

3. When you enter <Cmd sid='reportApp.dashboardSettings' product='2' /> mode, click <IcTrash2 /> in the upper right corner of the **Widget** you want to delete.

4. Check the delete confirmation pop-up and click <Cmd sid='reportApp.yes' product='2' />.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-dashboard-widget-del.png' className='none' />

5. Click <Cmd sid='reportApp.apply' product='2' /> in the upper right corner of the <Cmd sid='reportApp.dashboardSettings' product='2' /> screen.

Confirm that the changes made on the dashboard are reflected.
