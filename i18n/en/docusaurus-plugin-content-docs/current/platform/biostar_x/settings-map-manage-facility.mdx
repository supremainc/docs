Configure a facility and arrange floors. Arrange floors sequentially to represent the actual facility layout. When you configure facilities, the <Cmd sid='shell.header.arena' /> page lets you select facilities placed on the map and enter each floor for monitoring.

<Image src='/img/monitoring-map-select-floor.png' />

:::info

* Register floors before configuring facilities. For more information about registering floors, see <DocLink docId='platform/biostar_x/settings-map-manage-floor' />.

* For more information about map monitoring, see <DocLink docId='platform/biostar_x/monitoring-map' />.

* Adding and setting up facilities is available with an **Essential** license or higher. For more information about licensing policies, see <DocLink docId='platform/biostar_x/licensing#features-add-ons' />.

:::

## Add a facility

To design the structure by adding floors to a facility, follow these steps.

1. On the <Cmd sid='shell.header.launcher' /> page, click <Cmd sid='shell.header.setting' />.

2. Click <Cmd sid='setting.menu.map.root' /> → <Cmd sid='setting.menu.map.facility' /> in the left sidebar.

3. Click <Cmd sid='setting.facility.table.action.newFacility' /> in the upper-right corner of the facility list screen.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-settings-map-add-facility.png' />

4. In the <Cmd sid='setting.facility.information.section.title' /> section of the <Cmd sid='setting.facility.page.title' /> screen, enter the name and description for the facility to add.

5. In <Cmd sid='setting.facility.configuration.section.title' />, click <Cmd sid='common.add' />.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-settings-map-add-facility-config.png' />

6. When the <Cmd sid='setting.facility.dialog.floor.titleAddFloor' /> window appears, select the floor to add from the floor list.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-settings-map-facility-add-floor.png' />

   * Alternatively, enter a keyword in the input field to search for floors.

   * To select all floors, select <Cmd sid='setting.floor.table.title' />.

7. After you select the floors, click <Cmd sid='common.apply' />.

8. To review the floors added to the list and change their order, drag <IcDorder /> in the <Cmd sid='setting.facility.floorTableHeaders.order' /> column.

9. To complete the facility configuration and save, click <Cmd sid='button.apply' product='2' /> in the lower-right corner of the screen.

## Edit a facility

### Add floors to an existing facility

You can add floors to an existing facility.

1. On the <Cmd sid='shell.header.launcher' /> page, click <Cmd sid='shell.header.setting' />.

2. Click <Cmd sid='setting.menu.map.root' /> → <Cmd sid='setting.menu.map.facility' /> in the left sidebar.

3. From the facility list, select the facility to add floors to.

4. In <Cmd sid='setting.facility.configuration.section.title' />, click <Cmd sid='common.add' />.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-settings-map-add-facility-config-add.png' />

5. When the <Cmd sid='setting.facility.dialog.floor.titleAddFloor' /> window appears, select the checkbox for each floor to add.

6. After you select the floors, click <Cmd sid='common.apply' />.

7. To review the floors added to the list and change their order, drag <IcDorder /> in the <Cmd sid='setting.facility.floorTableHeaders.order' /> column.

8. To complete the facility configuration and save, click <Cmd sid='button.apply' product='2' /> in the lower-right corner of the screen.

### Delete floors from a facility

You can delete floors from an existing facility.

1. On the <Cmd sid='shell.header.launcher' /> page, click <Cmd sid='shell.header.setting' />.

2. Click <Cmd sid='setting.menu.map.root' /> → <Cmd sid='setting.menu.map.facility' /> in the left sidebar.

3. From the facility list, select the facility to delete floors from.

4. In <Cmd sid='setting.facility.configuration.section.title' />, click <IcClose /> for the floor to delete.

5. To complete the floor deletion and save, click <Cmd sid='button.apply' product='2' /> in the lower-right corner of the screen.

## Delete a facility

1. On the <Cmd sid='shell.header.launcher' /> page, click <Cmd sid='shell.header.setting' />.

2. Click <Cmd sid='setting.menu.map.root' /> → <Cmd sid='setting.menu.map.facility' /> in the left sidebar.

3. Select the checkbox for the facility to delete in the facility list.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-settings-map-remove-facility-list.png' />

4. Click <Cmd sid='common.delete' /> in the upper-right corner of the screen.

5. When the confirmation message appears, click <Cmd sid='common.dialog.yes' />.
