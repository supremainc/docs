Learn how to set up groups to easily manage multiple doors. A door group is a feature that lets you manage multiple doors as a single group. When you set up a group, you can change the settings of multiple doors at once.

:::tip

Register group names based on door locations or office names for easier management.

:::

## Add a door group {/* #adding-door-group */}

1. On the <Cmd sid='shell.header.launcher' /> page, click <Cmd sid='shell.header.setting' />.

2. Click <Cmd sid='setting.menu.accessPoint.root' /> → <Cmd sid='setting.menu.door.root' /> in the left sidebar.

3. In the door list, select <Cmd sid='menu.door.allDoorGroups' product='2' /> and right-click.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-setting-door-add-group.png' />

4. Click <Cmd sid='door.addDoorGroup' product='2' /> in the pop-up menu.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-setting-door-add-group-naming.png' />

5. Enter the group name.

:::info

* You can create up to eight levels of door groups.

* A door group name can be up to 48 characters long.

:::

## Rename a door group {/* #renaming-door-group */}

1. On the <Cmd sid='shell.header.launcher' /> page, click <Cmd sid='shell.header.setting' />.

2. Click <Cmd sid='setting.menu.accessPoint.root' /> → <Cmd sid='setting.menu.door.root' /> in the left sidebar.

3. In the door list, select the group to rename and right-click.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-setting-door-rename-group.png' />

4. Click <Cmd sid='door.renameDoorGroup' product='2' /> in the pop-up menu.

5. Enter the new group name.

:::info

A door group name can be up to 48 characters long.

:::

## Delete a door group {/* #deleting-door-group */}

1. On the <Cmd sid='shell.header.launcher' /> page, click <Cmd sid='shell.header.setting' />.

2. Click <Cmd sid='setting.menu.accessPoint.root' /> → <Cmd sid='setting.menu.door.root' /> in the left sidebar.

3. In the door list, select the group to delete and right-click.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-setting-door-rename-group.png' />

4. Click <Cmd sid='door.deleteDoorGroup' product='2' /> in the pop-up menu.

:::caution

Deleting a door group deletes all doors in the group. To avoid deleting the doors in the group, move the doors to another group, and then delete the group.

:::

## Add doors to a group {/* #adding-door-to-group */}

### Add to a group from the door list

1. On the <Cmd sid='shell.header.launcher' /> page, click <Cmd sid='shell.header.setting' />.

2. Click <Cmd sid='setting.menu.accessPoint.root' /> → <Cmd sid='setting.menu.door.root' /> in the left sidebar.

3. In the door list, select the door to add to a group and drag it to the target group.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-setting-door-to-group.png' />

The selected door moves to the group.

<Image src='/img/biostarx-setting-door-to-group-result.png' />

### Add to a group from door information

1. On the <Cmd sid='shell.header.launcher' /> page, click <Cmd sid='shell.header.setting' />.

2. Click <Cmd sid='setting.menu.accessPoint.root' /> → <Cmd sid='setting.menu.door.root' /> in the left sidebar.

3. In the door list in the right pane, click the door to add to a group.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-setting-door-to-group-list.png' />

4. When the door information edit screen appears, in the <Cmd sid='common.information' product='2' /> section, click the <Cmd sid='common.group' product='2' /> option.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-setting-door-to-group-info-select.png' />

5. Select the group to add the door to.

6. Click <Cmd sid='button.apply' product='2' /> at the bottom of the screen.
