Enroll a user's face as an authentication credential for access. Face enrollment uses a camera to capture the user's face as an authentication credential. You can also enroll the user's facial information captured by the camera remotely on a mobile device.

:::note

**Precautions when enrolling a face**

import EnrollingFace from '../../common/_enrolling-face-warning.mdx';

<EnrollingFace />

:::

:::info

You can also use Import from CSV, enroll multiple users' faces at once, or email a face enrollment link to multiple users so they can enroll their faces directly on a mobile device. For more information, see <DocLink docId='platform/biostar_x/batch-import-face' />.

:::

## Enroll by device {/* #enrolldevice */}

In the <Cmd sid='common.credential' product='2' /> section, click <Cmd sid='button.addFace' product='2' />. When the <Cmd sid='userCredential.enroll.visualFaceEnroll' product='2' /> window appears, set each field and click <Cmd sid='button.enroll' product='2' />.

<Image src='/img/biostarx-new-user-credential-face.png' />

* <Cmd sid='common.device' product='2' />: Select the device to enroll a face.

* <Cmd sid='button.addplus' product='2' />: Adds a face for enrollment. You can add up to two faces.

* <Cmd sid='button.scan' product='2' />: Click the added <Cmd>Nth</Cmd> button to scan the face. Follow the on-screen instructions on the device to scan the face.

  <Image src='/img/biostarx-new-user-credential-face-nth.png' />

* <Cmd sid='button.uploadImage' product='2' />: Uploads a face image without scanning a face.

  :::note

  * The maximum supported image file size is 10 MB.

  * Supported image formats are JPG, JPEG, and PNG.

  :::

* <Cmd sid='userCredential.enroll.useProfile' product='2' />: Select this checkbox to use the enrolled face image as the profile image.

* <Cmd sid='button.delete' product='2' />: Deletes a face credential. Select the number to delete and click Delete.

:::info

* For devices that can enroll faces, see below.

  * BioStation 3 Max

  * BioEntry W3

  * BioStation 3

  * FaceStation F2

* If the facial authentication rate is low, delete the face information and enroll a new face.

:::

## Enroll with a webcam or photo {/* #erollbywebcambyphoto */}

Take a photo with a webcam connected to your PC, set it as the user's profile image, and enroll the face as a credential. Alternatively, enroll an uploaded photo as a face credential.

1. Connect a webcam to your PC.

2. Hover over the profile image area in the upper-right corner of the screen and click <IcCamera /> that appears.

   To upload an image saved on your PC, click <IcUpload />.

   <Image src='/img/common/biostarx-new-user-photo.png' alone />

3. When the <Cmd sid='user.header.imageRegistration' product='2' /> window appears, click <Cmd sid='button.takePhoto' product='2' /> or <Cmd sid='button.uploadPhoto' product='2' />.

4. The photo appears when you take a photo with the webcam, and the uploaded photo appears when you upload a photo from the PC.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-new-user-credential-face-webcam-take.png' className='none' />

5. To enroll the photo taken with the webcam or the uploaded photo as a face credential, select the <Cmd sid='user.imageRegistration.useVisualFace' product='2' /> checkbox.

6. To complete facial enrollment, click <Cmd sid='button.enroll' product='2' />.

:::info

* When you use the webcam for the first time, a pop-up asking for permission to use the camera appears. To use the webcam, you must grant the browser permission to use the camera.

* The process of allowing camera permission may differ depending on the browser.

* It may take a few seconds to click <Cmd sid='button.takePhoto' product='2' /> and take the photo.

* It is recommended to use Google Chrome version 100 or later.

* The maximum image file size for upload is 10 MB.

* The supported image formats are JPG, JPEG, and PNG.

* When capturing a face with a webcam, make sure you follow the guidelines below.

  * Stay at a moderate distance from the webcam, not too close and not too far.

  * Look straight ahead and stay still while enrolling your face.

  * Make sure your facial expression does not change.

  * Don't wear a mask, hat, or eye patch.

  * Don't raise or lower your head.

  * Don't wear heavy makeup.

  * Don't close your eyes.

  * Make sure both shoulders are visible.

  * Make sure two faces do not appear at the same time. Capture one person at a time.

:::
