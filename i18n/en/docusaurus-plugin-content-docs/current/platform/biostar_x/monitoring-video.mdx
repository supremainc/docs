The video monitoring feature lets you monitor and control camera video linked to doors, areas, and devices in real time. Learn how to use video tiles to add camera video, resize, reposition, switch to full screen, and run other tasks. It also helps you respond quickly to security situations with management features such as checking and clearing alarm status.

:::info

* The **video tile** is the area in the center of the screen where camera video appears. It is an interface component that lets you place and monitor multiple camera videos at the same time. In a video tile, you can resize or reposition video, and close it or switch to full screen as needed.

  <Image src='/img/biostarx-monitoring-video-tiles.png' width='70%' />

* The <Cmd sid='arena.title' /> area is disabled if the system has no registered camera devices or no registered map components (areas, facilities, floors).

* For more information about integrating with VMS and adding and managing cameras, see <DocLink docId='platform/biostar_x/settings-videos' />.

* If a load error occurs when you play recorded video from the VMS, install a certificate on the VMS server and install the VMS server certificate on the client PC. For more information, see the following documents.

  * [Install the certificate on the VMS server](settings-video-integration#install-vms-certification)

  * [Install the VMS server certificate on the client PC](settings-video-integration#video-troubleshooting)

* If cameras associated with the area are not visible, check the camera group permissions. If an administrator with custom permissions has not set camera group permissions, cameras might not appear. For more information about setting camera group permissions, see <DocLink docId='platform/biostar_x/settings-videos-camera#addCameraPermission' />.

* Video monitoring is available as an additional option with the **Essential** license or above. For more information about licensing policies, see <DocLink docId='platform/biostar_x/licensing#features-add-ons' />.

:::

## Play video {/* #play-video */}

Add camera video to a video tile for monitoring. The video tile is located in the center of the screen, and it lets you monitor multiple camera videos at the same time.

1. Select the camera device you wish to view from the <Cmd sid='arena.tree.door.title' /> or <Cmd sid='arena.tree.map.title' />, <Cmd sid='arena.tree.device.title' /> tabs in the left sidebar.

2. Right-click.

3. Click <Cmd sid='arena.tree.contextMenu.openVideo' /> in the pop-up menu.

You can place video from the selected camera in the video tile in the center of the screen.

<Image src='/img/monitoring-door-control-open-video.png' />

:::info

* Alternatively, select a camera from the list in the sidebar and drag and drop it into the video tile area.

  <Image src='/img/monitoring-door-control-open-video-drag-drop.png' />

* Videos are added to the video tiles in order, starting in the upper-left corner of the screen; if the current screen has no space, an error message appears.

:::

## Control video {/* #control-video */}

Video placed in a video tile plays live by default. Use the playback tools to stop the video or view recorded video from an earlier time.

<Image src='/img/monitoring-video-player.png' alone />

<Calloutlists>

1. The name of the camera device.

2. These buttons play video in full screen (<Image src='/img/menus/ico-fullscreen.svg' ico alone />) or remove it from the video tile (<Image src='/img/menus/ico-close.svg' ico alone />).

3. This is a slider that allows you to adjust the playback position of the video. Drag the slider to view video from an earlier time.

4. To play or pause the video, click <Image src='/img/menus/ico-play.svg' ico alone /> or <Image src='/img/menus/ico-pause.svg' ico alone />.

5. This icon indicates that the current video is being played in real-time. To switch back to live video while playing video from a past time, click this icon.

</Calloutlists>

### Retain recorded video playback and playback failure notice {/* #recording-playback-failure */}

If you leave the <Cmd sid='arena.video.title' /> tab (for example, by going to another page, switching to the <Cmd sid='arena.tree.map.title' /> tab, or refreshing) and then return to the <Cmd sid='arena.video.title' /> tab, the recorded video you were playing keeps its playback state. Each video tile then reloads its video in the order it was placed.

:::info

Because response times vary by video, playback failure may appear at different times on each tile. However, playback failures tend to appear first on the tiles at the end of the tile order.

:::

## Door control tool {/* #door-control-tool */}

After you add video, use the <Cmd sid='arena.map.doorControl' /> tool to run additional tasks. When you select a camera linked to a door in a video tile, the <Cmd sid='arena.map.doorControl' /> tool to control the door becomes active. It supports door unlock, lock, normalize, and alarm clear features.

<Image src='/img/monitoring-door-control.png' alt='Door control' />

:::info

* The <Cmd sid='arena.map.doorControl' /> tool is available when nothing is open in the right pane. If detailed information is open in the right pane, close it before using the tool.

* This feature requires a camera to be linked to the door. For more information about linking cameras and doors, see <DocLink docId='platform/biostar_x/settings-videos-rule' />.

* For more information about the features available in the <Cmd sid='arena.map.doorControl' /> tool, see <DocLink docId='platform/biostar_x/control-door' />.

:::

## Check video alarm {/* #check-the-video-alarm */}

When one of the doors linked to the camera enters an alarm state, an alarm icon appears on the video. The alarm icon disappears only after all alarms for the linked doors are cleared.

<Image src='/img/monitoring-video-alarm.png' alt='Alarm' />

To clear the door alarm, right-click on the door in the list and click <Cmd sid='arena.tree.contextMenu.clearAlarm' /> in the pop-up menu. Alternatively, in the <Cmd sid='arena.map.doorControl' /> tool, click <Cmd sid='arena.tree.contextMenu.clearAlarm' />.

<Image src='/img/biostarx-monitoring-camera-clear-alarm.png' />

A success or failure message appears at the top of the screen.

<Image src='/img/biostarx-monitoring-camera-clear-alarm-success.png' />

:::info

For more information about the <Cmd sid='arena.eventTable.event' /> section at the bottom of the screen, see <DocLink docId='platform/biostar_x/monitoring-event' />.

:::

## Check video log {/* #check-video-log */}

When a specific event occurs at a door set by a rule, the corresponding event entry in the list in the <Cmd sid='arena.eventTable.title' /> section shows the video log (<IcVidLog />) icon. When you click an event entry with the video log icon, detailed information appears in the right pane. This lets you view video from when the event occurred.

<Image src='/img/biostarx-monitoring-camera-event-video-log.png' />

:::info

* The video log icon appears only when a camera is linked to the door. For more information about linking cameras and doors, see <DocLink docId='platform/biostar_x/settings-videos-rule' />.

* Based on the event setting rule, playback prioritizes the first linked camera. To play the video on a larger screen, double-click it.

* A door supports up to four linked cameras; click the camera button below the video to play video from the selected camera. If only one camera is linked, the camera button does not appear.

* For more information about the <Cmd sid='arena.eventTable.event' /> section at the bottom of the screen, see <DocLink docId='platform/biostar_x/monitoring-event' />.

:::

## Set video tile layout {/* #video-tile-layout-setting */}

Resize the video placed in a video tile or move it to a different location. Monitor by adjusting the size and position of video based on its importance.

### Change video size {/* #change-video-size */}

1. When you click the video to resize, the frame of the selected video becomes active.

   <Image src='/img/monitoring-video-adjust-size-01.png' alone />

2. When you hover the mouse cursor over the frame border, the cursor changes to a resize cursor.

3. Click a corner and drag it to the size you need. 

   <Image src='/img/monitoring-video-adjust-size-02.png' alone />

Resize the video in proportion to the tile size. You can also reduce the video size.

<Image src='/img/monitoring-video-adjust-size-03.png' alone />

### Enlarge video size {/* #video-size-enlargement */}

To enlarge the video to the full size of the video tile, double-click the video. The video expands to the size of the video tile.

<Image src='/img/common/monitoring-video-extend.gif' alone />

### Change video location {/* #changing-the-video-location */}

Click the video to move, and drag it to the new location. The selected video can be moved.

<Image src='/img/monitoring-video-move-position.png' alone />
