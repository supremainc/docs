Learn how to set up groups to easily manage multiple elevators. Elevator groups are a feature that lets you manage multiple elevators as a single group. When you set up a group, you can change the settings of multiple elevators at once.

:::tip

Registering group names based on elevator location makes management easier.

:::

## Add an elevator group {/* #adding-elevator-group */}

1. On the <Cmd sid='shell.header.launcher' /> page, click <Cmd sid='shell.header.setting' />.

2. Click <Cmd sid='setting.menu.accessPoint.root' /> → <Cmd sid='setting.menu.elevator.root' /> in the left sidebar.

3. Select <Cmd sid='elevator.main.top' product='2' /> in the elevator list, and right-click.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-setting-elevator-add-group.png' />

4. Click <Cmd sid='door.addDoorGroup' product='2' /> in the pop-up menu.

5. Enter the group name.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-setting-elevator-add-group-naming.png' />

:::info

* You can create up to eight levels of elevator groups.

* An elevator group name can be up to 48 characters long.

:::

## Rename an elevator group {/* #renaming-elevator-group */}

1. On the <Cmd sid='shell.header.launcher' /> page, click <Cmd sid='shell.header.setting' />.

2. Click <Cmd sid='setting.menu.accessPoint.root' /> → <Cmd sid='setting.menu.elevator.root' /> in the left sidebar.

3. Select the group to rename in the elevator list, and right-click.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-setting-elevator-add-group-re-naming.png' />

4. Click <Cmd sid='door.renameDoorGroup' product='2' /> in the pop-up menu.

5. Enter the new group name.

:::info

An elevator group name can be up to 48 characters long.

:::

## Delete an elevator group {/* #deleting-elevator-group */}

1. On the <Cmd sid='shell.header.launcher' /> page, click <Cmd sid='shell.header.setting' />.

2. Click <Cmd sid='setting.menu.accessPoint.root' /> → <Cmd sid='setting.menu.elevator.root' /> in the left sidebar.

3. Select the group to delete in the elevator list, and right-click.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-setting-elevator-add-group-re-naming.png' />

4. Click <Cmd sid='door.deleteDoorGroup' product='2' /> in the pop-up menu.

:::caution

If you delete an elevator group, all elevators in the group are deleted. To avoid deleting elevators in the group, move the elevators to another group, and then delete the group.

:::

## Add an elevator to a group {/* #adding-elevator-to-group */}

### Add an elevator to a group from the elevator list

1. On the <Cmd sid='shell.header.launcher' /> page, click <Cmd sid='shell.header.setting' />.

2. Click <Cmd sid='setting.menu.accessPoint.root' /> → <Cmd sid='setting.menu.elevator.root' /> in the left sidebar.

3. Select the elevator to add to the group in the elevator list, and drag it to the target group.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-setting-elevator-add-group-move.png' />

The selected elevator moves to the group.

### Add an elevator to a group from the elevator information

1. On the <Cmd sid='shell.header.launcher' /> page, click <Cmd sid='shell.header.setting' />.

2. Click <Cmd sid='setting.menu.accessPoint.root' /> → <Cmd sid='setting.menu.elevator.root' /> in the left sidebar.

3. In the elevator list in the right pane, click the elevator to add to the group.

4. When the elevator information edit screen appears, click the <Cmd sid='common.group' product='2' /> option in the <Cmd sid='common.information' product='2' /> section.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-setting-elevator-assign-group.png' />

5. Select the group to add the elevator to.

6. Click <Cmd sid='button.apply' product='2' /> at the bottom of the screen.
