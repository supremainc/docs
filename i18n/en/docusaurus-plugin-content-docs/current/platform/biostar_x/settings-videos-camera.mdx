After you complete VMS integration, you must select and set up the cameras to use in BioStar X. Proper camera settings enable the following security management features.

* **Selective monitoring**: Add only the cameras you need from all cameras in VMS to **BioStar X**.

* **Efficient management**: Group cameras to manage them systematically by location or purpose.

:::info

* For more information about how to integrate with the VMS server, see <DocLink docId='platform/biostar_x/settings-video-integration' />.

* Camera group permission is required to add cameras or set up groups. For more information about camera group permissions, see <DocLink inner='#addCameraPermission' />.

:::

## Set up and manage cameras

After VMS integration, you can set up and manage cameras in the <Cmd sid='shell.header.setting' /> → <Cmd sid='setting.menu.video.root' /> → <Cmd sid='setting.menu.video.video' /> menu.

### Add camera

If VMS is integrated, you can add cameras connected to the VMS server to **BioStar X** and play live and recorded video on the <Cmd sid='shell.header.arena' /> page.

1. On the <Cmd sid='shell.header.launcher' /> page, click <Cmd sid='shell.header.setting' />.

2. Click <Cmd sid='setting.menu.video.root' /> → <Cmd sid='setting.menu.video.video' /> in the left sidebar.

3. Click <Cmd sid='video.addCamera.button' product='2' />.

4. When the <Cmd sid='video.addCamera' product='2' /> window appears, select the cameras to exclude from the camera list.

   If a camera does not appear, click <Cmd sid='video.addCamera.refresh' product='2' /> to refresh the camera list.

5. Once you have selected the cameras, click <Cmd sid='button.add' product='2' /> in the lower-right corner of the screen.

The camera list shows the cameras you added.

<Image src='/img/biostarx-settings-video-add-camera.png' />

### Edit camera information

Edit information for the cameras you added. Change the camera name, group, and description.

1. On the <Cmd sid='shell.header.launcher' /> page, click <Cmd sid='shell.header.setting' />.

2. Click <Cmd sid='setting.menu.video.root' /> → <Cmd sid='setting.menu.video.video' /> in the left sidebar.

3. Click the <Cmd sid='video.table.th.camera' product='2' /> tab in the upper-left corner of the screen.

4. In the camera list, click the camera to edit.

5. When the screen showing camera information appears, edit the fields.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-settings-video-edit-camera-info.png' />

6. After editing all the camera information, click <Cmd sid='button.apply' product='2' /> in the lower-right corner of the screen.

:::info

The camera ID and IP address cannot be edited.

:::

### Delete a camera

Delete the cameras you added.

1. On the <Cmd sid='shell.header.launcher' /> page, click <Cmd sid='shell.header.setting' />.

2. Click <Cmd sid='setting.menu.video.root' /> → <Cmd sid='setting.menu.video.video' /> in the left sidebar.

3. In the camera list, select the checkbox on the far left of the camera to delete. You can select one or more cameras.

4. Click <IcTrash /> <Cmd sid='video.camera.delete' product='2' /> in the upper-right corner of the screen.

5. Check the message and click <Cmd sid='button.yes' product='2' />.

<Image src='/img/biostarx-settings-video-delete-camera.png' />

:::info

If the camera you are deleting is included in a rule, you cannot delete it. Remove the camera from the rule, and then try again. For more information about creating and managing rules, see <DocLink docId='platform/biostar_x/settings-videos-rule' />.

:::

## Set up and manage camera groups

Group multiple cameras to manage them systematically by location or purpose. Follow the steps below to create and manage camera groups.

### Set camera group permissions {/* #addCameraPermission */}

An administrator needs camera group permissions to add cameras or set up groups. Administrators, user administrators, and monitoring administrators have permission for all camera groups. If you need separate permission settings, add a custom permission to set camera group permissions.

1. On the <Cmd sid='shell.header.launcher' /> page, click <Cmd sid='shell.header.setting' />.

2. Click <Cmd sid='setting.menu.account.root' /> → <Cmd sid='setting.menu.account.account' /> in the left sidebar.

3. Click <Cmd sid='setting.account.addCustomOperator' product='2' />.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-settings-account-add-custom-permission.png' />

4. Enter a name and description for the new permission.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-settings-account-add-custom-permission-details.png' />

5. In <Cmd sid='settings.account.menu.detailSetting' product='2' />, under <Cmd sid='common.permission.module.cameraGroup' product='2' />, select the camera group to assign permissions to.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-settings-video-camera-add-custom-camera-group.png' />

   * To grant permissions for all camera groups, select <Cmd sid='setting.account.group.allCameraGroups' product='2' />.

   * To grant permissions for an individual camera group, select a group shown in the camera group list. You can select one or more groups.

6. In <Cmd sid='settings.account.addUser' product='2' />, click <Cmd sid='button.plusAdd' product='2' /> and select the users to grant permissions to.

7. To save the settings, click <Cmd sid='button.apply' product='2' /> at the bottom of the screen.

:::info

If you do not have permission for a camera group, you cannot view the cameras in that group.

:::

### Add a camera group

Add a camera group to manage multiple cameras as a group.

1. On the <Cmd sid='shell.header.launcher' /> page, click <Cmd sid='shell.header.setting' />.

2. Click <Cmd sid='setting.menu.video.root' /> → <Cmd sid='setting.menu.video.video' /> in the left sidebar.

3. Click the <Cmd sid='video.table.th.camera' product='2' /> tab in the upper-left corner of the screen.

4. Right-click <Cmd sid='video.camera.all.group' product='2' />.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-settings-video-add-camera-group.png' />

5. Click <Cmd sid='video.contextMenu.add.group' product='2' /> from the pop-up menu.

6. Enter a group name and press <Kbd>Enter</Kbd>.

:::info

* Camera groups can have up to eight levels of subgroups.

* Camera group names can be up to 48 characters.

* When you select a camera group in the camera list, only the cameras in that group appear in the list.

:::

### Add multiple cameras to a group

You can add one or more cameras to the group you added.

1. On the <Cmd sid='shell.header.launcher' /> page, click <Cmd sid='shell.header.setting' />.

2. Click <Cmd sid='setting.menu.video.root' /> → <Cmd sid='setting.menu.video.video' /> in the left sidebar.

3. In the camera list, select the checkbox on the far left of each camera to add to the group. You can select one or more cameras.

4. If you select more than one camera, <IcEditUL /> <Cmd sid='button.batchEdit' product='2' /> will be activated in the upper-right corner of the screen. Click <Cmd sid='button.batchEdit' product='2' />.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-settings-video-batch-edit-camera.png' />

5. When the <Cmd sid='button.batchEdit' product='2' /> window appears, click <Cmd sid='common.group' product='2' /> of the <IcEdit /> item.

6. Select a group from the group list.

7. Click <Cmd sid='button.apply' product='2' />.

:::info

In the camera list on the <Cmd sid='video.table.th.camera' product='2' /> tab, you can also add cameras to a group by drag and drop.

<Image src='/img/biostarx-settings-video-move-camera-group.png' alone />

:::

### Edit a camera group name

1. On the <Cmd sid='shell.header.launcher' /> page, click <Cmd sid='shell.header.setting' />.

2. Click <Cmd sid='setting.menu.video.root' /> → <Cmd sid='setting.menu.video.video' /> in the left sidebar.

3. Click <Cmd sid='video.table.th.camera' product='2' />.

4. Right-click the group to rename.

5. Click <Cmd sid='video.contextMenu.rename.group' product='2' /> from the pop-up menu.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-settings-video-rename-camera-group.png' />

6. Enter a group name and press <Kbd>Enter</Kbd>.

### Delete a camera group

1. On the <Cmd sid='shell.header.launcher' /> page, click <Cmd sid='shell.header.setting' />.

2. Click <Cmd sid='setting.menu.video.root' /> → <Cmd sid='setting.menu.video.video' /> in the left sidebar.

3. Click <Cmd sid='video.table.th.camera' product='2' />.

4. Right-click the group to delete.

5. Click <Cmd sid='video.contextMenu.delete.group' product='2' /> from the pop-up menu.

6. Check the warning message, and then click <Cmd sid='button.yes' product='2' />.

:::info

If the camera group contains cameras, you cannot delete the group. Move the cameras in the group to another group, or remove them from the group, and then try again.

:::

## List column settings

You can add, reorder, or hide columns shown in the list.

1. On the <Cmd sid='shell.header.launcher' /> page, click <Cmd sid='shell.header.setting' />.

2. Click <Cmd sid='setting.menu.video.root' /> → <Cmd sid='setting.menu.video.video' /> in the left sidebar.

3. In the upper-right corner of the <Cmd sid='video.header.all.camera' product='2' /> list, click <IcMoreW /> → <Cmd sid='columnSetting.dialog.header' product='2' />.

4. When the <Cmd sid='columnSetting.dialog.header' product='2' /> window appears, select or clear the checkbox for each column to show or hide it. To change the column order, drag and drop the columns.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-settings-video-column-layout.png' />

5. After you change all column settings, click <Cmd sid='button.ok' product='2' />.

:::info

To restore the default column settings, click <Cmd sid='columnSetting.button.defaultColumn' product='2' />.

:::
