Approve or modify visit requests. Administrators can also add or delete visitors directly.

## Approve a visit

Learn how to review visit requests and approve a visitor's access.

1. On the <Cmd sid='shell.header.launcher' /> page, click <Cmd sid='shell.header.visitor' />.

2. Click the visitor to approve in the visitor list.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-visitor-register-lists.png' />

3. After you review the visitor's visit information, edit any fields that need changes, and then click <Cmd sid='common.edit' product='2' />.

4. Click <Cmd sid='visitor.status.checkIn' product='2' /> in the lower-right corner of the screen.

5. Check the contents of <Cmd sid='visitor.visitRegisterInfo' product='2' /> and to approve the visit, click <Cmd sid='button.checkin' product='2' /> in the lower-right corner of the screen.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-visitor-register-item-approved.png' />

:::info

* If a visitor did not agree to the visitor access terms when requesting the visit, the <Cmd sid='button.checkin' product='2' /> button is disabled. Click <Cmd sid='visitor.term.watch' product='2' /> to provide the access terms to the visitor and obtain agreement. If the visitor does not agree to the visitor access terms, the visit is restricted.

  <Image src='/img/visitor-register-manage-view-term.png' />

* If a card enrollment device is set up on the visitor PC, the <Cmd sid='visitor.button.approveAndRegisterCard' product='2' /> button is enabled. Click <Cmd sid='visitor.button.approveAndRegisterCard' product='2' /> to approve the visitor and issue an access card. For more information, see <DocLink inner='#approve-and-register-card' />.

* To set up card usage on the visitor PC, click <Cmd sid='shell.header.setting' /> → <Cmd sid='shell.header.visitor' /> and set at least the following options. For more information, see <DocLink docId='platform/biostar_x/settings-visitor#settings-visitor' />.

  <Image src='/img/biostarx-visitor-settings-use-card.png' />

  * <Cmd sid='visitor.site' product='2' /> <Cmd sid='common.setting' product='2' /> → <Cmd sid='common.card' product='2' /> <Cmd sid='common.use' product='2' /> / <Cmd sid='user.cardType' product='2' />

  * <Cmd sid='visitor.pc.management' product='2' /> <Cmd sid='visitor.pc.setting' product='2' /> → <Cmd sid='common.card' product='2' /> <Cmd sid='setting.server.enrollment_device.device_name' product='2' />

:::

### Approve and enroll a card {/* #approve-and-register-card */}

Learn how to approve visit requests and issue access cards. 

1. In the <Cmd sid='visitor.visitRegisterInfo' product='2' /> screen, click <Cmd sid='visitor.button.approveAndRegisterCard' product='2' /> in the lower-right corner of the screen.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-visitor-register-item-approved-card.png' />

2. When the card enrollment window appears, select an enrollment method.

   * <Cmd sid='common.registerByCardReader' product='2' />

   * <Cmd sid='common.enterManually' product='2' />

#### <Cmd sid='common.registerByCardReader' product='2' className='normal' />

You can scan card information with a device connected to the visitor PC to enroll the card.

1. Select <Cmd sid='common.registerByCardReader' product='2' /> for <Cmd sid='common.registrationOption' product='2' />.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-visitor-register-item-approved-card-reader.png' />

2. To scan the card to enroll, click <Cmd sid='common.readCard' product='2' />.

3. To enroll the card, click <Cmd sid='button.enroll' product='2' />.

#### <Cmd sid='common.enterManually' product='2' className='normal' />

Enroll a card by entering the card number manually.

1. Select <Cmd sid='common.enterManually' product='2' /> for <Cmd sid='common.registrationOption' product='2' />.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-visitor-register-item-approved-card-manual.png' />

2. Enter <Cmd sid='user.cardId' product='2' /> manually.

3. To enroll the card, click <Cmd sid='button.enroll' product='2' />.

## Approve visitors with fingerprint search

Search for visitors with enrolled fingerprints, and then approve them.

1. On the <Cmd sid='shell.header.launcher' /> page, click <Cmd sid='shell.header.visitor' />.

2. Click <Cmd sid='visitor.button.searchVisitorByFingerprint' product='2' /> in the upper left of the visit request list.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-visitor-list-search-fingerprint.png' />

3. Scan the visitor's fingerprint on the fingerprint scanner.

4. When the system finds a visitor that matches the fingerprint, a confirmation message appears. Click <Cmd sid='visitor.button.registerInformation' product='2' />.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-visitor-list-search-fingerprint-confirm.png' />

5. After you review the visitor's visit information, edit any fields that need changes, and then click <Cmd sid='common.edit' product='2' />.

6. Click <Cmd sid='visitor.status.checkIn' product='2' /> in the lower-right corner of the screen.

7. Check the contents of <Cmd sid='visitor.visitRegisterInfo' product='2' /> and to approve the visit, click <Cmd sid='button.checkin' product='2' /> in the lower-right corner of the screen.

## Add a visitor

Learn how to add visitors directly as an administrator.

1. On the <Cmd sid='shell.header.launcher' /> page, click <Cmd sid='shell.header.visitor' />.

2. Click <IcAdd /> <Cmd sid='button.func.addVisitor' product='2' /> in the upper-left corner of the visitor list.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-visitor-register-lists-add.png' />

3. Enter the visitor's name and phone number in the <Cmd sid='common.visitor' product='2' /> section.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-visitor-register-lists-add-visitor-info.png' />

4. Enter the host's name and phone number in the <Cmd sid='visitor.host' product='2' /> section.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-visitor-register-lists-add-admin-info.png' />

   :::note

   When you enter a name or phone number, a list of users that match the entered information appears. To designate the desired user, click <Cmd sid='button.choose' product='2' />.

   :::

5. Set the access group and visit period in the <Cmd sid='visitor.accessInfo' product='2' /> section.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-visitor-register-lists-add-access-info.png' />

   :::note

   You can select only the access groups of the site assigned to the visitor PC. For more information, see <DocLink docId='platform/biostar_x/settings-visitor#settings-visitor-site' />.

   :::

6. Set the credential in the <Cmd sid='common.credential' product='2' /> section.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-visitor-register-lists-add-credential.png' />

   * <Cmd sid='common.card' product='2' />: To use card authentication, set it to <Cmd sid='visitor.request' product='2' />.

   * <Cmd sid='common.fingerPrint' product='2' />: To use fingerprint authentication, click <Cmd sid='button.addFingerprint' product='2' /> and then enroll the visitor's fingerprint.

7. To complete adding the visitor, click <Cmd sid='button.register' product='2' /> in the lower-right corner of the screen.

:::info

* The name can be up to 48 characters.

* For more information about adding custom fields to visitor information, see <DocLink docId='platform/biostar_x/settings-visitor#settings-visitor-custom-field' />.

:::

## Delete a visitor

1. On the <Cmd sid='shell.header.launcher' /> page, click <Cmd sid='shell.header.visitor' />.

2. In the visit request list, select the checkbox for the visitor to delete.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-visitor-register-lists-remove-visitor.png' />

3. Click <Cmd sid='button.func.deleteVisitor' product='2' /> in the upper-right corner of the screen.

4. When the confirmation message appears, click <Cmd sid='button.yes' product='2' />.

:::info

* The <Cmd sid='button.func.deleteVisitor' product='2' /> button is enabled when you select a visitor's checkbox.

* Visitors can be deleted only from the visit request list.

:::
