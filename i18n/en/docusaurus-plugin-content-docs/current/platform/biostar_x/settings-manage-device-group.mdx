Learn how to use the device group feature to efficiently manage multiple devices. Device groups let you manage multiple devices as one group, which is useful for applying device settings all at once or monitoring devices in large systems.

:::tip

Name device groups after the device location or purpose to make management easier. For example, you can name groups "1st Floor Entrance," "2nd Floor Office," and "Conference Room."

:::

## Create a device group

1. On the <Cmd sid='shell.header.launcher' /> page, click <Cmd sid='shell.header.setting' />.

2. Click <Cmd sid='setting.menu.device.root' /> → <Cmd sid='setting.menu.device.device' /> in the left sidebar.

3. Right-click <Cmd sid='menu.device.allDeviceGroups' product='2' /> in the device list.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-setting-device-add-group.png' />

4. Click <Cmd sid='device.menu.addDeviceGroup' product='2' /> in the pop-up menu.

5. Enter the group name.

:::info

* Device groups can have up to eight levels of subgroups.

* Device group names can be up to 48 characters long.

* When you select a device group in the device list, only the devices in that group appear in the list.

:::

## Rename a device group

1. On the <Cmd sid='shell.header.launcher' /> page, click <Cmd sid='shell.header.setting' />.

2. Click <Cmd sid='setting.menu.device.root' /> → <Cmd sid='setting.menu.device.device' /> in the left sidebar.

3. Under <Cmd sid='menu.device.allDeviceGroups' product='2' /> in the device list, right-click the device group to rename.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-setting-device-rename-group.png' />

4. Click <Cmd sid='device.menu.renameDeviceGroup' product='2' /> in the pop-up menu.

5. Enter the new group name.

:::info

Device group names can be up to 48 characters long.

:::

## Delete a device group

1. On the <Cmd sid='shell.header.launcher' /> page, click <Cmd sid='shell.header.setting' />.

2. Click <Cmd sid='setting.menu.device.root' /> → <Cmd sid='setting.menu.device.device' /> in the left sidebar.

3. Under <Cmd sid='menu.device.allDeviceGroups' product='2' /> in the device list, right-click the device group to delete.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-setting-device-remove-group.png' />

4. Click <Cmd sid='device.menu.deleteDeviceGroup' product='2' /> in the pop-up menu.

:::caution

When you delete a device group, all devices in the group are deleted.

:::
