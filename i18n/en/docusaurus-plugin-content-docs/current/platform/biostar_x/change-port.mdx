Learn how to change the port when the default port (443) for **BioStar X** is unavailable.

1. In Windows, click <Start/> → **BioStar X** → **BioStar X Service Manager**.

2. When the **BioStar X Service Manager** window appears in the web browser, click <Cmd>SERVICES</Cmd>.

3. To stop all services, click <Cmd>Stop All</Cmd> in the upper-right corner of the screen.

   <Image src='/img/common/biostarx-tutoria-service-manager-services-stop.png' alone />

4. Click <Cmd>SERVICE SETTINGS</Cmd> in the left sidebar.

5. In the <Cmd>Unified Gateway Service</Cmd> section, enter the new port number in the <Cmd>HTTPS Port</Cmd> field.

   <Image src='/img/common/biostarx-tutoria-service-manager-services-change-port.png' alone />

6. Click <Cmd>Apply</Cmd> in the upper-right corner of the screen.

7. When the confirmation message appears, click <Cmd>OK</Cmd>.

8. Navigate to <Cmd>SERVICES</Cmd> and click **Start** for all services.

9. Access **BioStar X** in a web browser.

:::info

* If you changed the port number to 450, enter `https://{ip_address}:450` in the address bar.

* Some services may not support changing the port.

:::
