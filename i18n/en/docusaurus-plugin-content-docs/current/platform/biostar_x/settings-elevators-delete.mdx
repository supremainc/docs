This guide describes how to delete registered elevators.

1. Click <Cmd sid='shell.header.setting' /> on the <Cmd sid='shell.header.launcher' /> page.

2. Click <Cmd sid='setting.menu.accessPoint.root' /> → <Cmd sid='setting.menu.elevator.root' /> on the left sidebar.

3. Click the checkbox of the elevator you want to delete from the elevator list.

4. Click <Cmd sid='elevator.list.deleteButton' product='2' /> at the top right of the screen.

The selected elevator will be deleted. Deleted elevators cannot be recovered.

:::info

<Cmd sid='elevator.list.deleteButton' product='2' /> activates only after selecting one or more elevators in the elevator list.

:::
