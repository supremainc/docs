Set up floors before you set up areas and facilities. Upload the floor plan for the floor as an image, and place doors and cameras to manage them visually. After you set up a floor, the <Cmd sid='shell.header.arena' /> page shows the doors and cameras for that floor.

<Image src='/img/common/biostarx-settings-map-add-floor-example.png' alone caption />

:::info

* Register doors before you set up floors. For more information about registering doors, see <DocLink docId='platform/biostar_x/settings-door-add' />.

* For more information about map monitoring, see <DocLink docId='platform/biostar_x/monitoring-map' />.

* Adding and setting up floors is available with an **Essential** license or higher. For more information about licensing policies, see <DocLink docId='platform/biostar_x/licensing#features-add-ons' />.

:::

## Add a floor {/* #add-floor */}

Upload a floor plan or 3D bird's-eye view image to add a floor and place doors and cameras.

1. On the <Cmd sid='shell.header.launcher' /> page, click <Cmd sid='shell.header.setting' />.

2. Click <Cmd sid='setting.menu.map.root' /> → <Cmd sid='setting.menu.map.floor' /> in the left sidebar.

3. Click <Cmd sid='setting.floor.table.actions.newFloor' /> in the upper-right corner of the floor list screen.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-settings-map-add-floor.png' />

4. In the <Cmd sid='setting.floor.information.section.title' /> section of the <Cmd sid='setting.floor.page.title' /> screen, enter the name and description of the floor to add.

5. In the <Cmd sid='setting.floor.configuration.section.title' /> section, click <Cmd sid='setting.floor.configuration.button.floorPlan' /> or drag the image to upload.

   :::note

   Supported image formats are PNG, JPEG, and JPG.

   :::

6. In the <Cmd sid='setting.floor.configuration.dialog.title.addFloorPlan' /> window, confirm the uploaded image and set the options.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-settings-map-add-floor-01.png' alone />

   * To rotate the image, click <IcLrot /> or <IcRrot />.

   * To crop the image, click <IcCrop />.

7. After you finish setting up the image, click <Cmd sid='common.apply' />.

8. To add a door to the uploaded image in the <Cmd sid='setting.floor.configuration.section.title' /> section, right-click the location to place the door in the image and select <Cmd sid='setting.floor.configuration.dialog.title.addDoor' />.

9. When the <Cmd sid='setting.floor.configuration.dialog.title.addDoor' /> window appears, select one of the registered doors.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-settings-map-add-floor-03.png' />

10. To place the door on the image, click <Cmd sid='common.apply' />.

    <Image src='/img/biostarx-settings-map-add-floor-04.png' alone />

11. To add a camera to the uploaded image, right-click the location to place the camera in the image and select <Cmd sid='setting.floor.configuration.dialog.title.addCamera' />.

12. When the <Cmd sid='setting.floor.configuration.dialog.title.addCamera' /> window appears, select one of the registered cameras.

    <Image src='/img/biostarx-settings-map-add-floor-05.png' />

13. To place the camera on the image, click <Cmd sid='common.apply' />.

    <Image src='/img/biostarx-settings-map-add-floor-06.png' alone />

14. Place additional doors and cameras installed on the floor.

15. To save the configured floor after you complete all settings, click <Cmd sid='button.apply' product='2' />.

:::info

* To move a door or camera placed on the floor, click and drag the door or camera to a new location.

* For more information about the icons and status of the doors and cameras on the floor, see <DocLink docId='platform/biostar_x/monitoring-map#component-and-options' />.

:::

## Edit a floor

You can edit the floor plan of a floor where doors and cameras are placed. Editing the floor plan lets you move or delete doors and cameras.

1. On the <Cmd sid='shell.header.launcher' /> page, click <Cmd sid='shell.header.setting' />.

2. Click <Cmd sid='setting.menu.map.root' /> → <Cmd sid='setting.menu.map.floor' /> in the left sidebar.

3. In the floor list, click the floor to edit.

To save the configured floor after you complete all settings, click <Cmd sid='common.apply' />.

### Delete a door or camera

Click <IcFremove /> for the door or camera you want to delete from the floor image. You can delete the door or camera.

### Set camera coverage

In the floor image, right-click the camera to set its coverage. Click <Cmd sid='setting.area.button.addCoverage' /> in the pop-up menu.

<Image src='/img/biostarx-settings-map-floor-add-camera-coverage.png' />

* To change the coverage, move the mouse pointer over the green area. When the mouse pointer changes to a crosshair, click and hold the mouse button, then drag to the target location.

  <Image src='/img/common/biostarx-settings-floor-camera-rotation.png' alone />

* To adjust the coverage, click and drag an end corner of the range to resize it.

  <Image src='/img/common/biostarx-settings-floor-camera-adjust.png' alone />

* To delete the coverage area, click <IcFremove />.

:::caution

The coverage you set is only for easier security management and does not reflect the camera's actual coverage.

:::

### Delete floor image

To delete the floor image, right-click on the floor image and select <Cmd sid='setting.floor.contextMenu.deleteFloor' /> from the pop-up menu.

<Image src='/img/biostarx-settings-map-remove-floor.png' />

## Delete a floor {/* #delete-floor */}

1. On the <Cmd sid='shell.header.launcher' /> page, click <Cmd sid='shell.header.setting' />.

2. Click <Cmd sid='setting.menu.map.root' /> → <Cmd sid='setting.menu.map.floor' /> in the left sidebar.

3. In the floor list, select the checkbox for the floor to delete. You can select one or more floors.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-settings-map-remove-floor-item.png' />

4. Click <Cmd sid='common.delete' /> in the upper-right corner of the screen.

The selected floors are deleted from the floor list. Deleted floors cannot be recovered.
