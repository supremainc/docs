**Quick Action** is a feature that automates complex control procedures during emergencies. Execute the actions of multiple predefined devices instantly with a single button to respond quickly.

After you add a quick action button in the <Cmd sid='setting.menu.customInterface.root' /> menu, click it on the **BioStar X** main screen to control multiple doors at once.

:::info

To add <Cmd sid='setting.menu.customInterface.root' /> to use as a quick action, see <DocLink docId='platform/biostar_x/settings-triger-and-action' />.

:::

## Add a quick action button

1. On the <Cmd sid='shell.header.launcher' /> page, click <Cmd sid='shell.header.setting' />.

2. Click <Cmd sid='setting.menu.accessManagement.root' /> → <Cmd sid="setting.menu.customInterface.root" /> in the left sidebar.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-settings-custom-interface.png' />

3. In the <Cmd sid="common.quickActionLayout" product='2' /> section, click <Cmd sid="button.plusAdd" product='2' />.

4. When the <Cmd sid='settings.customInterface.quickAction.add' product='2' /> window appears, enter and set each field.

   <Image src='/img/settings-custom-interface-add-quick-action.png' className='none' />

   * <Cmd sid="settings.customInterface.quickAction.operatorLevel" product='2' />: Select the <Cmd sid="settings.customInterface.quickAction.operatorLevel" product='2' /> that can run the quick action to add. Multiple selection is supported.

   * <Cmd sid="settings.customInterface.quickAction.triggerAction" product='2' />: Select the <Cmd sid="settings.customInterface.quickAction.triggerAction" product='2' /> to run. Multiple selection is supported.

   * <Cmd sid="settings.customInterface.quickAction.confirmBeforeRun" product='2' />: Enabling this option will prompt a confirmation pop-up when you press the <Cmd sid="common.quickAction" product='2' /> button to ask if you want to execute it.

5. To save the settings, click <Cmd sid="button.apply" product='2' />.

6. Click <Cmd sid="button.apply" product='2' /> in the lower-right corner of the screen. 

The added <Cmd sid="common.quickAction" product='2' /> button appears in the upper-right header area of the **BioStar X** screen. Click the <Cmd sid="common.quickAction" product='2' /> you created to verify it works as set in <Cmd sid='setting.menu.customInterface.root' />.

<Image src='/img/settings-custom-interface-main-quick-action.png' className='none' />

:::info

* The header area at the top of the screen can hold up to four <Cmd sid="common.quickAction" product='2' /> buttons. They stay pinned so that they are available on every page.

* Available <Cmd sid="common.quickAction" product='2' /> buttons may differ depending on the <Cmd sid="user.bioStarOperator" product='2' /> set for the user.

:::

## Edit a quick action button

1. On the <Cmd sid='shell.header.launcher' /> page, click <Cmd sid='shell.header.setting' />.

2. Click <Cmd sid='setting.menu.accessManagement.root' /> → <Cmd sid="setting.menu.customInterface.root" /> in the left sidebar.

3. Click <IcEditUL /> on the right side of the quick action button you want to modify.

   <Image src='/img/settings-custom-interface-quick-action-layout.png' />

4. When the pop-up for editing appears, modify the desired item and click <Cmd sid="button.apply" product='2' />.

5. To save the modifications, click <Cmd sid="button.apply" product='2' /> in the lower-right corner of the screen.

## Change the quick action order

1. On the <Cmd sid='shell.header.launcher' /> page, click <Cmd sid='shell.header.setting' />.

2. Click <Cmd sid='setting.menu.accessManagement.root' /> → <Cmd sid="setting.menu.customInterface.root" /> in the left sidebar.

3. In the quick action list, click and hold the leftmost column of the entry to move, then drag it to the new position.

   <Image src='/img/settings-custom-interface-quick-action-change-order.png' />

4. To save the new order, click <Cmd sid="button.apply" product='2' /> in the lower-right corner of the screen.

The quick action list in the upper-right corner of the screen shows the updated order.

<Image src='/img/settings-custom-interface-quick-action-change-order-result.png' />

## Pin buttons to the header

Place quick actions that you need to run quickly in the header area.

1. On the <Cmd sid='shell.header.launcher' /> page, click <Cmd sid='shell.header.setting' />.

2. Click <Cmd sid='setting.menu.accessManagement.root' /> → <Cmd sid="setting.menu.customInterface.root" /> in the left sidebar.

3. To place a quick action in the header area, click <Cmd sid='settings.customInterface.quickAction.pinOnHeader' product='2' />(<IcPin />).

   <Image src='/img/settings-custom-interface-quick-action-pin-header.png' />

   :::note

   Click and hold the leftmost column, then drag it to a new position to change the order, or turn off <Cmd sid='settings.customInterface.quickAction.pinOnHeader' product='2' />.

   :::

4. After you select all the entries to pin to the header area, click <Cmd sid="button.apply" product='2' /> in the lower-right corner of the screen.

Pinned quick actions appear to the left of the <Cmd sid='shell.header.action' /> button in the upper-right corner of the screen.

<Image src='/img/settings-custom-interface-quick-action-pin-header-result.png' />

:::info

* You can pin up to four quick actions to the header area. When four quick actions are already pinned, you cannot pin more. Unpin another entry, then try again.

* When the browser window narrows, header buttons disappear, and pinned quick actions appear with the pin (<IcPin fill='#d8403a' />) icon in the <Cmd sid='shell.header.action' /> button drop-down list.

* If all added quick actions are pinned to the header, the <Cmd sid='shell.header.action' /> button does not appear. If you unpin some entries, the button appears again.

:::

## Delete a quick action button

1. On the <Cmd sid='shell.header.launcher' /> page, click <Cmd sid='shell.header.setting' />.

2. Click <Cmd sid='setting.menu.accessManagement.root' /> → <Cmd sid="setting.menu.customInterface.root" /> in the left sidebar.

3. Click <IcTrash2 /> to the right of the quick action button to delete.

   <Image src='/img/settings-custom-interface-quick-action-layout.png' />

4. When the delete confirmation pop-up appears, click <Cmd sid="button.yes" product='2' />.

   <Image src='/img/settings-custom-interface-confirm.png' className='none' />

5. To reflect the deleted items, click <Cmd sid="button.apply" product='2' /> in the lower-right corner of the screen.
