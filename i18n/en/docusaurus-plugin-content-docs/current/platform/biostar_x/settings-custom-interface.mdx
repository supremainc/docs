**Quick Action** is a feature that automates complex control procedures during emergencies. Execute the actions of multiple predefined devices instantly with a single button to respond quickly.

Add a quick action button in the <Cmd sid='setting.menu.customInterface.root' /> menu. Click it on the **BioStar X** main screen to control multiple doors at once.

:::info

To add the <Cmd sid='setting.menu.customInterface.root' /> as a Quick Action, see <DocLink docId='platform/biostar_x/settings-triger-and-action' />.

:::

## Add quick action button

1. Click <Cmd sid='shell.header.setting' /> on the <Cmd sid='shell.header.launcher' /> page.

2. Click <Cmd sid='setting.menu.accessManagement.root' /> → <Cmd sid="setting.menu.customInterface.root" /> on the left sidebar.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-settings-custom-interface.png' />

3. Click <Cmd sid="button.plusAdd" product='2' /> in the <Cmd sid="common.quickActionLayout" product='2' /> section.

4. When the <Cmd sid='settings.customInterface.quickAction.add' product='2' /> window appears, enter and set each item.

   <Image src='/img/settings-custom-interface-add-quick-action.png' className='none' />

   * <Cmd sid="settings.customInterface.quickAction.operatorLevel" product='2' />: Select the <Cmd sid="settings.customInterface.quickAction.operatorLevel" product='2' /> that can execute the quick action you want to add. You can select multiple options.

   * <Cmd sid="settings.customInterface.quickAction.triggerAction" product='2' />: Select the <Cmd sid="settings.customInterface.quickAction.triggerAction" product='2' /> to execute. You can select multiple options.

   * <Cmd sid="settings.customInterface.quickAction.confirmBeforeRun" product='2' />: Enabling this option will prompt a confirmation popup when you press the <Cmd sid="common.quickAction" product='2' /> button to ask if you want to execute it.

5. Click <Cmd sid="button.apply" product='2' /> to save the settings.

6. Click <Cmd sid="button.apply" product='2' /> at the bottom right of the screen. 

The <Cmd sid="common.quickAction" product='2' /> button added in the upper right header area of the **BioStar X** screen will be created. Click the created <Cmd sid="common.quickAction" product='2' /> button to verify it works as configured in <Cmd sid='setting.menu.customInterface.root' />.

<Image src='/img/settings-custom-interface-main-quick-action.png' className='none' />

:::info

* You can add up to four <Cmd sid="common.quickAction" product='2' /> buttons in the upper header area of the screen. It is fixed for use on any page.

* The <Cmd sid="common.quickAction" product='2' /> buttons available may differ depending on the settings configured for the user <Cmd sid="user.bioStarOperator" product='2' />.

:::

## Edit Quick Action button

1. Click <Cmd sid='shell.header.setting' /> on the <Cmd sid='shell.header.launcher' /> page.

2. Click <Cmd sid='setting.menu.accessManagement.root' /> → <Cmd sid="setting.menu.customInterface.root" /> on the left sidebar.

3. Click the <IcEditUL /> button on the right side of the quick action button you want to modify.

   <Image src='/img/settings-custom-interface-quick-action-layout.png' />

4. When the popup for editing appears, modify the desired item and click the <Cmd sid="button.apply" product='2' /> button.

5. To save the modifications, click the <Cmd sid="button.apply" product='2' /> button at the bottom right of the screen.

## Change Quick Action order

1. Click <Cmd sid='shell.header.setting' /> on the <Cmd sid='shell.header.launcher' /> page.

2. On the left sidebar, click <Cmd sid='setting.menu.accessManagement.root' /> → <Cmd sid="setting.menu.customInterface.root" />.

3. In the quick action list, click and hold the leftmost column, then drag it to the desired position.

   <Image src='/img/settings-custom-interface-quick-action-change-order.png' />

4. Click <Cmd sid="button.apply" product='2' /> in the bottom right corner of the screen to save the new order.

The quick action list at the top right of the screen shows the updated order.

<Image src='/img/settings-custom-interface-quick-action-change-order-result.png' />

## Pin buttons to the header

Place quick actions that require quick access in the header area.

1. Click <Cmd sid='shell.header.setting' /> on the <Cmd sid='shell.header.launcher' /> page.

2. On the left sidebar, click <Cmd sid='setting.menu.accessManagement.root' /> → <Cmd sid="setting.menu.customInterface.root" />.

3. Click <Cmd sid='settings.customInterface.quickAction.pinOnHeader' product='2' />(<IcPin />) to pin the quick action to the header area.

   <Image src='/img/settings-custom-interface-quick-action-pin-header.png' />

   :::note

   Drag the leftmost column to reorder. Click <Cmd sid='settings.customInterface.quickAction.pinOnHeader' product='2' /> again to unpin.

   :::

4. Select all quick actions to pin to the header area. Click <Cmd sid="button.apply" product='2' /> in the bottom right corner.

Pinned quick actions appear to the left of the <Cmd sid='shell.header.action' /> button at the top right of the screen.

<Image src='/img/settings-custom-interface-quick-action-pin-header-result.png' />

:::info

* Pin up to 4 quick actions to the header area. When 4 quick actions are already pinned, you cannot pin more. Unpin another entry, then try again.

* When the browser window narrows, all individual buttons in the header disappear, and you can find the pinned quick actions with the pin (<IcPin fill='#d8403a' />) icon in the dropdown list of the <Cmd sid='shell.header.action' /> button.

* If all added quick actions are pinned to the header area, the <Cmd sid='shell.header.action' /> button does not appear. Unpin certain entries to show the button again.

:::

## Delete Quick Action button

1. Click <Cmd sid='shell.header.setting' /> on the <Cmd sid='shell.header.launcher' /> page.

2. Click <Cmd sid='setting.menu.accessManagement.root' /> → <Cmd sid="setting.menu.customInterface.root" /> on the left sidebar.

3. Click <IcTrash2 /> to the right of the quick action button you want to delete.

   <Image src='/img/settings-custom-interface-quick-action-layout.png' />

4. Check the deletion confirmation pop-up and click <Cmd sid="button.yes" product='2' />.

   <Image src='/img/settings-custom-interface-confirm.png' className='none' />

5. To reflect the deleted items, click <Cmd sid="button.apply" product='2' /> at the bottom right of the screen.
