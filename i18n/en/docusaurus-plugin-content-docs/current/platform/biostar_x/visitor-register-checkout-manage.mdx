View checked-out visitors and use their visit request information to register another visit.

1. On the <Cmd sid='shell.header.launcher' /> page, click <Cmd sid='shell.header.visitor' />.

2. Click the <Cmd sid='visitor.status.checkOut' product='2' /> tab at the top of the screen.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-visitor-register-checkout-list.png' />

3. To register a visitor again, click the visitor in the visitor list.

4. Click <Cmd sid='common.edit' product='2' /> in the lower-right corner of the screen.

5. If there are items that need correction, modify the individual items and then click <Cmd sid='button.enroll' product='2' />.

<Image src='/img/biostarx-visitor-register-checkout-edit.png' />

The re-registered visitor is added to the visit request list. To check, click the <Cmd sid='visitor.status.register' product='2' /> tab at the top of the screen.
