Learn how to edit a widget added to the dashboard.

1. On the <Cmd sid='shell.header.launcher' /> page, click <Cmd sid='shell.header.dashboard' />.

2. Click <IcSet2 /> in the upper-right corner of the screen. 

3. In <Cmd sid='reportApp.dashboardSettings' product='2' /> mode, click <IcEdit /> in the upper-right corner of the **widget** to edit.

4. When the <Cmd sid='reportApp.editWidget' product='2' /> window appears, edit the fields.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-widget-edit.png' className='none' caption />

5. When you finish editing, click <Cmd sid='reportApp.apply' product='2' />.

6. On the <Cmd sid='reportApp.dashboardSettings' product='2' /> screen, click <Cmd sid='reportApp.apply' product='2' /> in the upper-right corner of the screen.

Confirm that the changes you made on the dashboard screen are reflected.

:::info

* In <Cmd sid='reportApp.editWidget' product='2' />, you cannot change <Cmd sid='reportApp.widgetTypes' product='2' />.

* <Cmd sid='reportApp.widgetTypes.text' product='2' />, <Cmd sid='reportApp.widgetTypes.systemUsage' product='2' />, and <Cmd sid='reportApp.widgetTypes.doorControl' product='2' /> use fixed entries, so you cannot edit <Cmd sid='reportApp.widgetName' product='2' />.

:::
