Advanced access control settings let you control access. You can configure various advanced access control features based on security requirements and your operating environment. You can prevent unauthorized re-entry with anti-passback, respond to emergencies such as fire or intrusion, control doors automatically based on schedules, or limit and monitor the number of people in specific areas.
