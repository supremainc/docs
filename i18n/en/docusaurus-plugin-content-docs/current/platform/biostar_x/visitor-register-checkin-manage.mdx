You can view checked-in visitors, edit visit information, or check them out.

1. On the <Cmd sid='shell.header.launcher' /> page, click <Cmd sid='shell.header.visitor' />.

2. Click the <Cmd sid='visitor.status.checkIn' product='2' /> tab at the top of the screen.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-visitor-register-checkin-list.png' />

3. To edit or check out a visitor, click the visitor in the list.

4. Review the visitor's visit request information.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-visitor-register-checkin-edit.png' />

   * To modify and save individual items, click <Cmd sid='common.edit' product='2' /> in the lower-right corner of the screen.

   * To process check-out, click <Cmd sid='button.checkout' product='2' /> in the lower-right corner of the screen.

:::info

You can also select a visitor from the <Cmd sid='visitor.status.checkIn' product='2' /> list to check out the visitor. Click the checkbox for the visitor to check out in the list and then click <Cmd sid='button.checkout' product='2' /> in the upper-right corner of the screen.

<Image src='/img/biostarx-visitor-register-checkin-list-checkout.png' />

:::
