To sign in to BioStar Air with Microsoft Entra ID, an ID administrator creates an app registration in Entra ID. Then the administrator links it to the BioStar Air SSO settings.

## Connect Entra ID SSO

<Steps>
<Step>

### BioStar Air: Prepare redirect URI {/* #bsairRedirect */}

1. Click <Cmd sid='settings_menu' product='air' /> → <Cmd sid='site_submenu' product='air' /> in the left sidebar of BioStar Air.

2. Click the <Cmd>SSO & SCIM</Cmd> tab.

3. Under <Cmd sid='sso_scim.additional_features.sso.identity_provider' product='air' />, select <Cmd sid='sso_scim.additional_features.sso.microsoft_entra_id' product='air' />.

4. Copy the URI value shown in the <Cmd sid='sso_scim.additional_features.sso.redirect_uri' product='air' /> field.

<Image src='/img/air/bsair-settings-site-idp-entraid.png' />

</Step>
<Step>

### Microsoft Entra admin center: Register app {/* #appRegistration */}

1. Sign in to the [Microsoft Entra admin center](https://entra.microsoft.com/).

2. Go to **Entra ID** → **App registrations**.

3. Click **New registration**.

   <Image src='/img/air/bsair-sso-entra-app-reg.png' alone />

4. Enter 'BioStar Air' for the app name.

5. Under **Supported account types**, select **Single tenant only**.

6. In **Redirect URI**, select **Web**. Paste the URI from step <DocLink inner='#bsairRedirect' /> into the field.

   <Image src='/img/air/bsair-sso-entra-app-reg-new.png' alone />

7. Click **Register**.

8. In the BioStar Air app **Overview**, copy the following values.

   <Image src='/img/air/bsair-sso-entra-app-overview.png' alone />

   * **Application (client) ID**

   * **Directory (tenant) ID**

9. Under **Certificates & secrets** → **Client secrets**, click **New client secret**.

   <Image src='/img/air/bsair-sso-entra-app-certificates-secrets.png' alone />

10. Enter a description and select an expiration period.

    <Image src='/img/air/bsair-sso-entra-app-add-client-secret.png' alone />

11. Click **Add**.

12. Copy the **Value** of the generated client secret.

    <Image src='/img/air/bsair-sso-entra-app-copy-client-secret.png' alone />

Enter the copied **Application (client) ID**, **Directory (tenant) ID**, and **Value** in the next step.

:::caution

Use **Value**, not **Secret ID**. The **Value** appears only once, so copy it now.

:::

</Step>
<Step>

### Microsoft Entra admin center: Assign users and groups

Create users and groups before assigning them.

1. In **Enterprise apps**, click the app created earlier (BioStar Air).

   <Image src='/img/air/bsair-sso-entra-enterprise-apps.png' alone />

2. Click **Assign users and groups**.

   <Image src='/img/air/bsair-sso-entra-enterprise-apps-overview.png' alone />

3. Click **Add user/group** to assign users and groups.

   <Image src='/img/air/bsair-sso-entra-enterprise-apps-users-groups.png' alone />

4. After assigning users and groups, click **Assign**.

</Step>
<Step>

### BioStar Air: Configure SSO

1. Return to the BioStar Air Settings screen and enter the values copied in <DocLink inner='#appRegistration' /> into each field.

   <Image src='/img/air/bsair-settings-site-idp-entraid-sso-config.png' />

   * <Cmd sid='sso_scim.additional_features.sso.client_id' product='air' />: **Application (client) ID**

   * <Cmd sid='sso_scim.additional_features.sso.client_secret' product='air' />: **Value**

   * <Cmd sid='sso_scim.additional_features.sso.tenant_id' product='air' />: **Directory (tenant) ID**

2. In the <Cmd sid='sso_scim.additional_features.sso.email_domain' product='air' /> field, enter your organization's email domain.

3. Click <Cmd sid='sso_scim.additional_features.sso.save' product='air' />.

After the system saves and verifies successfully, it automatically generates the value for <Cmd sid='sso_scim.additional_features.sso.discovery_url' product='air' />.

<Image src='/img/air/bsair-settings-site-idp-entraid-sso-config-success.png' />

</Step>
</Steps>

## Connect Entra ID SCIM provisioning

<Steps>
<Step>

<CopyToken />

</Step>
<Step>

### Microsoft Entra admin center: Create a SCIM provisioning app

1. In **Enterprise apps**, click **New application**.

   <Image src='/img/air/bsair-sso-entra-enterprise-apps-new.png' alone />

2. Click **Create your own application**.

   <Image src='/img/air/bsair-sso-entra-enterprise-apps-new-own.png' alone />

3. Enter the app name as 'BioStar Air SCIM'.

4. Select **Integrate any other application you don't find in the gallery (Non-gallery)**.

   <Image src='/img/air/bsair-sso-entra-enterprise-apps-new-own-create.png' alone />

5. Click **Create**.

6. In the BioStar Air SCIM app, click **Manage** → **Provisioning**.

   <Image src='/img/air/bsair-sso-entra-scim-app-overview.png' alone />

</Step>
<Step>

### Microsoft Entra admin center: Set admin credentials and start provisioning

1. Click **New Configuration**.

   <Image src='/img/air/bsair-sso-entra-scim-app-provisioning.png' alone />

2. In **Admin credentials**, enter the SCIM endpoint URL and token copied in <DocLink inner='#copyToken' /> into **Tenant URL** and **Secret token**.

   <Image src='/img/air/bsair-sso-entra-scim-app-new-config.png' alone />

3. Click **Test Connection** to test synchronization.

4. Click **Create**.

5. In **Users and groups**, click **Add user/group**.

   <Image src='/img/air/bsair-sso-entra-scim-app-users-groups.png' alone />

6. Assign the users and security groups to provision.

7. Click **Assign**.

8. Go to **Provisioning** → **Mappings** and confirm that user and group synchronization is enabled.

   <Image src='/img/air/bsair-sso-entra-scim-app-mapping.png' alone />

9. Review the mappings before changing attributes.

10. In the SCIM app **Overview**, click **Start provisioning**.

    <Image src='/img/air/bsair-sso-entra-scim-app-start-provisioning.png' alone />

:::info

It is recommended to start with a small test group. BioStar Air can take up to 40 minutes to reflect group membership changes.

:::

## Sign in with Entra ID

After completing Entra ID SSO connection, users sign in to BioStar Air with a Microsoft account on the sign-in screen.

1. On the BioStar Air sign-in screen, click <Cmd sid='login_with_microsoft_account' product='air' />.

2. Select <Cmd sid='sso_scim.login.microsoft_org_dialog.microsoft_entra' product='air' />.

   <Image src='/img/air/bsair-login-with-entra-id.png' />

   :::note

   If your organization already uses Microsoft SSO, select <Cmd sid='sso_scim.login.microsoft_org_dialog.microsoft_sso' product='air' /> to sign in without additional settings.

   :::

3. Enter the organization's email address or domain in the <Cmd sid='sso_scim.login.sso_scim_org_dialog.label' product='air' /> field. (For example, `user@company.com` or `company`)

4. Click <Cmd sid='sso_scim.login.sso_scim_org_dialog.continue' product='air' />.

5. On the Microsoft Entra ID sign-in screen, enter your organization's account email address and password.

6. After authentication is complete, you are automatically redirected to BioStar Air, completing the sign-in process.

:::info

If Entra ID SSO is not set up for the entered email domain, an error message appears. Contact the site administrator.

:::
