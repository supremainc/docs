## Group mapping {/* #groupMapping */}

After connecting SSO and SCIM and pushing IdP groups, the site administrator must map them to BioStar Air groups.

### Mapping rules {/* #mappingRule */}

* You can map multiple IdP groups to a single BioStar Air user group.

* A BioStar Air user group must be empty before mapping.

* BioStar Air groups with members cannot serve as mapping targets.

* Only the site master administrator can manage IdP group mapping.

* The system locks the role after mapping an IdP group to the master role.

### Map administrator roles and user groups

1. Click <Cmd sid='settings_menu' product='air' /> → <Cmd sid='site_submenu' product='air' /> in the left sidebar of BioStar Air.

2. Click the <Cmd>SSO & SCIM</Cmd> tab.

3. Go to the <Cmd sid='sso_scim.group_mapping.title' product='air' /> section and click <Cmd sid='add_btn' product='air' />.

   <Image src='/img/air/bsair-settings-site-idp-okta-group-mapping.png' />

4. From <Cmd sid='sso_scim.group_mapping.idp_group' product='air' />, select an IdP group.

5. In <Cmd sid='sso_scim.group_mapping.mapping_target' product='air' />, select <Cmd sid='sso_scim.group_mapping.admin_role' product='air' /> or <Cmd sid='sso_scim.group_mapping.local_user_group' product='air' /> based on the selected IdP group.

6. Depending on the mapping target, assign an administrator role or select a user group.

   <Image src='/img/air/bsair-settings-site-idp-okta-group-mapping-add.png' className='none' />

7. Click <Cmd sid='save_btn' product='air' /> to save the mapping.

The <Cmd sid='sso_scim.group_mapping.title' product='air' /> section shows IdP groups and their mapped BioStar Air groups.

<Image src='/img/air/bsair-settings-site-idp-okta-mappinged-groups.png' />
