### BioStar Air: Check the connection

1. Click <Cmd sid='settings_menu' product='air' /> → <Cmd sid='site_submenu' product='air' /> in the left sidebar of BioStar Air.

2. Click the <Cmd>SSO & SCIM</Cmd> tab.

3. In the <Cmd sid='sso_scim.additional_features.scim.title' product='air' /> section, check for the <Cmd sid='sso_scim.additional_features.scim.status_connected' product='air' /> indicator.
