To sign in to BioStar Air with Okta, the ID administrator must create an OIDC application in Okta. Link it to the SSO settings in BioStar Air.

## Connect Okta SSO

<Steps>
<Step>

### BioStar Air: Prepare redirect URI {/* #bsairRedirect */}

1. Click <Cmd sid='settings_menu' product='air' /> → <Cmd sid='site_submenu' product='air' /> in the left sidebar of BioStar Air.

2. Click the <Cmd>SSO & SCIM</Cmd> tab.

3. Select <Cmd sid='sso_scim.additional_features.sso.okta' product='air' /> under <Cmd sid='sso_scim.additional_features.sso.identity_provider' product='air' />.

4. Copy the URI value shown in the <Cmd sid='sso_scim.additional_features.sso.redirect_uri' product='air' /> field.

<Image src='/img/air/bsair-settings-site-idp-okta.png' />

</Step>
<Step>

### Okta: Create an app

1. Sign in to the **Okta Admin Console**.

2. Go to **Applications and Resources** → **Applications**.

   <Image src='/img/air/bsair-sso-okta-applications.png' alone />

3. Click **Create App Integration**.

4. Under **Sign-in method**, select **OIDC - OpenID Connect**.

5. Under **Application type**, select **Web Application** so you can obtain a client secret (Client secret).

   <Image src='/img/air/bsair-sso-okta-applications-create-app.png' alone />

6. Click **Next**.

7. In **App Integration name**, enter 'BioStar Air'.

8. Paste the URI copied in the <DocLink inner='#bsairRedirect' /> step into the input field under **Sign-in redirect URIs**.

   <Image src='/img/air/bsair-sso-okta-applications-create-app-general-settings.png' alone />

9. Go to **Assignments**, then select **Allow everyone in your organization to access**.

   <Image src='/img/air/bsair-sso-okta-applications-create-app-assignments.png' alone />

10. Click **Save**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Okta: Copy app client information {/* #copyClientinfo */}

1. After creating the application, copy **Client ID** and **Client Secret** from the **General** tab.

   <Image src='/img/air/bsair-sso-okta-applications-general.png' alone />

2. Click the profile in the upper-right corner, then copy **Okta Domain**.

   <Image src='/img/air/bsair-sso-okta-applications-profile.png' alone />

Enter the copied **Client ID**, **Client Secret**, and **Okta Domain** in the next step.

</Step>
<Step>

### BioStar Air: Configure SSO

1. Return to the BioStar Air settings page, then enter the values copied in the <DocLink inner='#copyClientinfo' /> step into each field.

   <Image src='/img/air/bsair-settings-site-idp-okta-paste-field.png' />

   * <Cmd sid='sso_scim.additional_features.sso.client_id' product='air' />: **Client ID**

   * <Cmd sid='sso_scim.additional_features.sso.client_secret' product='air' />: **Client Secret**

   * <Cmd sid='sso_scim.additional_features.sso.okta_domain' product='air' />: **Okta Domain**

2. In the <Cmd sid='sso_scim.additional_features.sso.email_domain' product='air' /> field, enter your organization's email domain.

3. Click <Cmd sid='sso_scim.additional_features.sso.save' product='air' />.

After the system saves and verifies successfully, it automatically generates the value for <Cmd sid='sso_scim.additional_features.sso.discovery_url' product='air' />.

<Image src='/img/air/bsair-settings-site-idp-okta-save-success.png' />

</Step>
</Steps>

## Connect Okta SCIM provisioning

<Steps>
<Step>

<CopyToken />

</Step>
<Step>

### Okta: Create a SCIM provisioning app

1. In the **Okta Admin Console**, go to **Applications and Resources** → **Applications**.

   <Image src='/img/air/bsair-sso-okta-applications-create-provisioning-app.png' alone />

2. Click **Browse App Catalog**.

3. Search for 'SCIM 2.0 Test App'.

   <Image src='/img/air/bsair-sso-okta-applications-browse-app-search.png' alone />

4. In the search results, select <b>SCIM 2.0 Test App (Header Auth)</b>.

5. Click **Add Integration**.

6. In the **Application label** field, enter 'BioStar Air SCIM'.

   <Image src='/img/air/bsair-sso-okta-scim-app-general.png' alone />

7. Click **Next**.

8. In **Sign-on options**, scroll down without changing settings, and click **Done**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Okta: API integration settings

1. After creating the provisioning app, click the **Provisioning** tab.

   <Image src='/img/air/bsair-sso-okta-scim-app-provisioning.png' alone />

2. Click **Configure API Integration**.

3. Click **Enable API integration**.

4. Enter the SCIM endpoint URL and SCIM bearer token from <DocLink inner='#copyToken' /> into the **Base URL** and **API Token** fields.

   <Image src='/img/air/bsair-sso-okta-scim-app-provisioning-set-api.png' alone />

5. To test synchronization, click **Test API Credentials**.

6. Click **Save**.

7. Click the **Provision** tab to set group push behavior.

8. Click **Edit**, then enable the following attributes.

   <Image src='/img/air/bsair-sso-okta-scim-app-provisioning-set-push.png' alone />

   * **Create Users**

   * **Update User Attributes**

   * **Deactivate Users**

9. Click **Save** to save the changes.

   <Image src='/img/air/bsair-sso-okta-scim-app-provisioning-save.png' alone />

</Step>
</Steps>

## Push IdP groups to BioStar Air

To push IdP groups to BioStar Air, first prepare user groups and users in Okta. After preparing user groups and users, push them to BioStar Air via SCIM.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Okta: Assign user groups

1. Sign in to the **Okta Admin Console**.

2. Go to **Applications and Resources** → **Applications**.

3. Select the SCIM application.

   <Image src='/img/air/bsair-sso-okta-applications-list.png' alone />

4. Click the **Assignments** tab.

5. Click **Assign** → **Assign to Groups**.

   <Image src='/img/air/bsair-sso-okta-scim-app-assign-groups.png' alone />

6. In the user group to assign to the SCIM application, click **Assign**.

7. After assigning the group, click **Done**.

   <Image src='/img/air/bsair-sso-okta-scim-app-assigned-group-list.png' alone />

</Step>
<Step>

### Okta: Push user groups

1. Click the **Push Groups** tab.

2. Click **Push Groups** → **Find groups by name**.

   <Image src='/img/air/bsair-sso-okta-scim-app-push-groups.png' alone />

3. In the input field, enter the group name to push. 

4. When a matching user group appears, select it.

   <Image src='/img/air/bsair-sso-okta-scim-app-push-groups-by-name.png' alone />

5. After selecting a user group, click **Save** or **Save & Another** to add it.

6. Check the pushed user groups on the **Push Groups** tab.

   <Image src='/img/air/bsair-sso-okta-scim-app-pushed-group-list.png' alone />

</Step>

## Sign in to Okta

After completing the Okta SSO connection, users can sign in with their Okta accounts on the BioStar Air login screen.

1. On the BioStar Air login screen, click <Cmd sid='sso_scim.login.sign_in_with_okta' product='air' />.

   <Image src='/img/air/bsair-login-with-okta.png' />

2. Enter the organization's email address or domain in the <Cmd sid='sso_scim.login.sso_scim_org_dialog.label' product='air' /> field. (For example, `user@company.com` or `company`)

3. Click <Cmd sid='sso_scim.login.sso_scim_org_dialog.continue' product='air' />.

4. When the Okta sign-in page opens, enter the email address and password for the organization account.

5. After authentication is complete, you are automatically redirected to BioStar Air, completing the sign-in process.

:::info

If Okta SSO is not set up for the entered email domain, an error message appears. Contact the site administrator.

:::
