title: Safety
description: This section covers the Safety Management feature in BioStar Air. It checks on-site users and verifies evacuation during emergencies.
---

BioStar Air’s Safety Management features help track people on site during normal operations and manage evacuations during emergencies. Presence, including **On-Site Now** and **Zones**, gives administrators a real-time view of which credential holders are on site and where they are within the site. Administrators can also manually update a person’s presence status.

Roll Call supports orderly evacuations by allowing designated administrators to take responsibility for specific muster points and oversee the safety of the people assigned to them. It uses the same on-site presence engine as **On-Site Now**.
