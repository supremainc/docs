This guide explains how to reset the camera to factory settings and replace the desiccant module.

## Factory reset {/* #factory-reset */}

Deletes all information stored on the camera and resets all settings.

1. After connecting the camera to power, locate the reset button.

   <Image src='/img/camera/factory-reset-vionyx.svg' className='none' width={350} alone />

2. Press and hold the reset button for at least 5 seconds, then release it.

3. After the factory reset completes, the camera will restart automatically.

:::caution

Performing a factory reset will delete all user data and settings, including network settings.

:::

:::info

* By default, the IP address is configured via DHCP.

* If the device does not get an IP address after a factory reset, it changes to the default IP address (`https://192.168.1.100`), so connect using that address.

:::

## Replace the desiccant module {/* #change-silicagel */}

To maintain dehumidification performance, we recommend replacing the desiccant module at least every 1 year.

1. Remove the used desiccant module from the cover.

   <Image src='/img/camera/installation-desiccant-module1-vionyx.svg' className='none' width={480} alone />

2. Remove the protective film from the back of the new desiccant module.

   <Image src='/img/camera/installation-desiccant-module2-vionyx.svg' className='none' width={350} alone />

3. Align the groove on the desiccant module with the protrusion on the cover. Attach the module, and press it by hand until it fully adheres.

   <Image src='/img/camera/installation-desiccant-module3-vionyx.svg' className='none' width={700} alone />

:::caution

* The desiccant module comes in sealed packaging. Open the packaging immediately before attaching it, as exposure to air may reduce dehumidifying performance.

* Replacement intervals may vary depending on the installation environment.

:::

:::info

* Record the replacement date to keep track of the next replacement.

* Replacement desiccant modules can be purchased from the retailer where you bought the product.

:::
