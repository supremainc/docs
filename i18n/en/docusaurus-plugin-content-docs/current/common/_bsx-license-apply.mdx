1. Log in with the **BioStar X** administrator account.

2. On the <Cmd sid='shell.header.launcher' /> page, click <Cmd sid='shell.header.setting' />.

3. Click <Cmd sid='setting.menu.license.root' /> → <Cmd sid='setting.menu.license.biostarXLicense' /> in the left sidebar.

4. In <Cmd sid='setting.xlicense.activation' product='2' />, enter <Cmd sid='setting.xlicense.activatedBy' product='2' />, and then enter <Cmd sid='setting.xlicense.activationKey' product='2' />.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-tutorial-license-online.png' />

5. Click <Cmd sid='setting.server.license.button.activate' product='2' />.

Registered licenses appear in <Cmd sid='setting.xlicense.myLicense' product='2' />.

:::info

To activate a license in a closed network environment or offline with limited internet access, see <DocLink docId='platform/biostar_x/settings-license-biostar-x-license#registering-offline' />.

:::
