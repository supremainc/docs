Saved reports are stored as templates, so you can reuse them to generate reports with the same conditions.

:::info

* To edit the title, events, period, and filter conditions of a saved report, click <IcRepoEdit /> at the top of the report.

* You can also change the report title under <Cmd sid='data.sidebar.savedReports' /> in the left sidebar. Right-click the custom report to rename. Click <Cmd sid='data.sidebar.renameSavedReport' /> in the pop-up menu.

  <Image src='/img/biostarx-data-custom-report-rename.png' />

* For more information about managing reports, see <DocLink inner='#report-management' />.

:::
