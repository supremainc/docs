## End roll call {/* #endRollCall */}

You can end an ongoing roll call in both the **BioStar X** mobile app and on the web.

### End in the mobile app

1. To end a roll call, tap <IcRCstop title='End' width={20} height={20} /> in the roll call list screen, or tap <Cmd>END</Cmd> in the roll call status screen.

   <Image src='/img/common/biostarx-settings-adv-ac-mobile-app-rollcall-end.png' className='none' width='600px' alone />

2. If unaccounted users remain, a warning pop-up appears. To end the roll call, enter an end reason and tap <Cmd>Yes</Cmd>.

   <Image src='/img/common/biostarx-settings-adv-ac-mobile-app-rollcall-end-unaccounted.png' width='272px' alone />

### End on the web

1. On the <Cmd sid='shell.header.launcher' /> page, click <Cmd sid='shell.header.setting' />.

2. Click <Cmd sid='setting.menu.accessManagement.root' /> → <Cmd sid='setting.menu.advancedAc.root' /> in the left sidebar.

3. Click <Cmd sid='zone.rollCall' product='2' /> from the list.

4. In the roll call list, select the checkbox for the roll call to end.

   <Image src='/img/biostarx-settings-adv-ac-rollcall-stop.png' />

5. Click <Cmd sid='zone.pause.end' product='2' /> in the upper-right corner of the screen.

6. When the confirmation message appears, click <Cmd sid='button.yes' product='2' />.

A roll call with no unaccounted users ends immediately. For a roll call with unaccounted users, a dialog appears with the <Cmd sid='zone.rollCall.unaccountedUsers' product='2' /> list and the <Cmd sid='zone.rollCall.endReason' product='2' /> field. To end the roll call, enter a reason and click <Cmd sid='zone.rollCall.endButton' product='2' />.

<Image src='/img/biostarx-settings-adv-ac-rollcall-stop-unaccounted-users.png' />

:::info

* <Cmd sid='zone.rollCall.endReason' product='2' />: Enter up to 500 characters.

* You cannot end multiple roll calls at once if any of them has unaccounted users. End roll calls one at a time.

:::
